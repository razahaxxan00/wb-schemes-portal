Popular & Featured Schemes

Top searched West Bengal government welfare programs with complete guides.

Women Welfare 👩

Lakshmir Bhandar Scheme

Financial aid of Rs 1,000 (General) and Rs 1,200 (SC/ST) monthly for women in West Bengal. Check eligibility and status.

View Scheme Details & Apply →
Health Insurance 🏥

Swasthya Sathi Scheme

Rs 5 lakh cashless health insurance per family per year in secondary and tertiary hospitals across West Bengal.

View Scheme Details & Apply →
Farmer Welfare 🌾

Krishak Bandhu Scheme

Rs 10,000 annual financial assistance per acre and Rs 2 lakh death assurance for farmers in West Bengal.

View Scheme Details & Apply →
Girl Child Aid 👧

Kanyashree Prakalpa

K1 annual scholarship and K2 one-time grant of Rs 25,000 for unmarried girl students to pursue education.

View Scheme Details & Apply →
Housing 🏠

Banglar Bari Prakalpa

Subsidized housing and flat allotment scheme for economically weaker sections (EWS) in urban West Bengal.

View Scheme Details & Apply →
Minority Scholarship 🕌

Aikyashree Scholarship

Financial support for minority students (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Parsee, Jain) from Class I to PhD level.

View Scheme Details & Apply →
