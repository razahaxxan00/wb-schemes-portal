Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Overview & Disclaimer

West Bengal Schemes Portal (wbschemes.in) operates as an independent public informational resource providing guides, eligibility criteria, document requirements, and status checking instructions for West Bengal government schemes. We are NOT affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by the Government of West Bengal or any official department.

2. Information Collection & Usage

We respect your privacy. Our website does not collect sensitive personal identifier numbers (such as Aadhaar, Voter ID, Ration Card, or Bank Account numbers). Any status check or form fill-up forms on our website redirect users to official government portals (e.g. swasthyasathi.gov.in, krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in).

3. Log Files & Analytics Cookies

Like most standard websites, wbschemes.in utilizes web analytics tools (such as Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity) to analyze trends, administer the site, and track user navigation at an aggregated, anonymized level.

  • IP address (anonymized)
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Referring pages and duration of visit

4. Data Security

We implement sitewide HTTPS encryption (SSL) and follow standard security practices to protect all browsing activity on our website.

5. External Links

Our articles and scheme guides contain direct links to official government websites (.gov.in / .wb.gov.in). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external third-party sites.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Us page or email us at contact@wbschemes.in.
