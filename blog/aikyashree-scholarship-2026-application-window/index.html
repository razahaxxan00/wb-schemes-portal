Aikyashree Scholarship 2026 — Application Window Now Open

The Aikyashree Scholarship application cycle for the current academic year is open, covering Pre-Matric, Post-Matric, and Merit-cum-Means (MCM) categories for students from West Bengal's notified minority communities. Here's what applicants and renewal students need to know this cycle.

Who Can Apply This Cycle

Aikyashree covers students from West Bengal's notified minority communities — Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, and Parsi — across three linked categories depending on class or course:

  • Pre-Matric — for school students before Class 10
  • Post-Matric — Class 11 through postgraduate, diploma, ITI, and PhD level
  • Merit-cum-Means (MCM) — professional and technical courses such as engineering, medicine, management, and law

Key Things to Know This Cycle

Separate deadlines by category. Fresh applications, renewals, Post-Matric, MCM, and TSP (Talent Support Programme) each have their own last date, announced by WBMDFC at the start of the cycle. Don't assume one deadline covers every category, check the specific date for your category on the official portal.

Renewal applicants should verify bank details first. A large share of renewal delays trace back to a bank account that's no longer active or an Aadhaar link that's broken since the last cycle, rather than any issue with the application itself. Confirming this before submitting saves a round of correction later.

Check your status before submitting a duplicate application. If you're unsure whether you already have an active application from a previous cycle, use the official portal's "Track Your Application" feature first. Submitting a fresh application when one already exists can create duplicate records and processing delays.

How to Apply (Summary)

  1. Visit the official WBMDFC/Aikyashree scholarship portal
  2. Choose Fresh Registration, Renewal, or log in if already registered
  3. Select your district and the applicable category (Pre-Matric, Post-Matric, or MCM)
  4. Fill in personal, academic, and bank account details
  5. Upload required documents — minority/community certificate, income certificate, previous mark sheet, Aadhaar card, bank passbook, and institution/bonafide certificate
  6. Submit and note your Application ID for future status tracking

Why Deadlines Vary Every Year

Aikyashree doesn't run on a single fixed academic calendar date each year, WBMDFC announces the specific opening and closing dates for each category at the start of every cycle, based on administrative and budgetary planning for that year. This is why relying on last year's dates, or a date shared informally on social media, can lead to missing the actual current window. The safest approach is always checking the live notice on the official portal itself.

What Happens If You Miss a Deadline

In past cycles, WBMDFC has sometimes reopened windows specifically for renewal applicants after the initial deadline passed, though this isn't guaranteed every year. If you've missed a deadline, checking the official portal's notice board for any extension announcement is worthwhile before assuming you'll need to wait for the entire next academic cycle.

Checking Your Application Status

Once submitted, you can track your application through the official portal's status-check tool using your district and Application ID. See our full Aikyashree Status Check guide for the complete step-by-step process, including what to do if your application appears stuck or shows no status.

For complete eligibility criteria and income limits, visit our main Aikyashree Scholarship guide.
