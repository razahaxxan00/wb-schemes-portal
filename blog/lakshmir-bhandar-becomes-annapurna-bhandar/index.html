Lakshmir Bhandar Is Now Annapurna Bhandar — Here's What Changed

Following the 2026 West Bengal Assembly election and the formation of a new state government, Lakshmir Bhandar, the monthly cash assistance scheme for women, has been restructured and renamed Annapurna Bhandar, with rollout starting June 2026. Here's a complete breakdown of what changed and what existing beneficiaries need to do.

What Lakshmir Bhandar Was

Lakshmir Bhandar (লক্ষ্মীর ভান্ডার) launched in 2021, providing monthly direct benefit transfer payments to eligible women from economically weaker West Bengal households. Eligible women were aged 25 to 60, not government employees, pensioners, or income-tax payers. At its peak, around 2.2 crore women were enrolled, receiving ₹1,500/month (General category) or ₹1,700/month (SC/ST category).

What Changed With Annapurna Bhandar

  • Monthly amount increased to ₹3,000 for all eligible women, replacing the earlier General/SC-ST split entirely, a flat rate regardless of category
  • Existing beneficiaries are being migrated automatically after a re-verification exercise, in most cases without needing to submit a fresh application
  • A new application window opened for women who weren't previously enrolled under Lakshmir Bhandar
  • Some previously enrolled women were removed after an eligibility audit, commonly for reasons like government employment, pension receipt, or a broken Aadhaar-bank linkage that couldn't be verified

Why the Name and Structure Changed

The renaming followed the change in state government after the 2026 Assembly election. The new administration restructured the scheme with a higher benefit amount and a simplified single-tier payment structure, rebranding it as Annapurna Bhandar as part of that broader policy shift.

If You Were Already Receiving Lakshmir Bhandar

Most existing beneficiaries continue receiving monthly payments under the new scheme name without needing to reapply. The transition primarily involved:

  • A re-verification exercise to confirm continued eligibility
  • Automatic migration of verified beneficiaries into the new Annapurna Bhandar payment system
  • Updated payment amount (₹3,000) reflected from the point of migration onward

If Your Payment Has Stopped

If you were previously receiving Lakshmir Bhandar and your payments have stopped, the most likely explanations are:

  • Failed re-verification — if your details couldn't be confirmed during the transition audit
  • Aadhaar-bank linkage issues — a broken or outdated link between your Aadhaar and bank account
  • Change in eligibility status — such as new government employment, a pension, or a family member's income-tax status affecting the household

None of these necessarily mean the scheme has ended for you personally, checking your specific status is the right first step before assuming the worst.

How to Check Your Status

Use the official West Bengal Social Security / Annapurna Bhandar portal's "Track Applicant & View Payment Status" tool with your Application ID, Swasthya Sathi card number, or registered mobile number. See our full Lakshmir Bhandar Status Check guide for the complete step-by-step process, including what to do about common payment issues.

Applying Fresh Under Annapurna Bhandar

If you weren't previously enrolled in Lakshmir Bhandar and believe you now qualify under Annapurna Bhandar's criteria, a new application window has been opened specifically for this purpose. Check the official portal for current application requirements and documentation, since these may differ slightly from the original Lakshmir Bhandar application process given the restructured eligibility framework.

Why We're Keeping the Old Scheme Name on This Site

Millions of West Bengal residents still search for information using the "Lakshmir Bhandar" name, since that's the scheme they originally enrolled under. Rather than only publishing content under the new "Annapurna Bhandar" name and leaving searchers confused, we've kept our main guide under the familiar name while clearly explaining the transition, so anyone searching either name finds accurate, current information. Visit our main Lakshmir Bhandar Scheme guide for complete eligibility and application details.
