Manabik Pension Amount Revision 2026 — What's Confirmed So Far

Reports have circulated that the Manabik Pension Scheme's monthly amount may be revised from ₹1,000 to ₹1,500, effective August 2026. This update covers what's confirmed, what remains unverified, and what beneficiaries should do while the situation develops.

Current Confirmed Amount

Manabik Pension Scheme, West Bengal's monthly pension for persons with disabilities, currently pays ₹1,000 per month to eligible beneficiaries via Direct Benefit Transfer, as part of the broader Jai Bangla Pension Scheme umbrella that consolidates the state's various pension programmes under one system.

What's Being Reported

Some sources have suggested a revision to ₹1,500 per month effective August 2026, as part of a wider review of pension amounts across Jai Bangla's sub-schemes. This would represent a 50% increase if confirmed and implemented as described.

Why We're Treating This Cautiously

At the time of this update, we have not found an official government notification confirming this exact figure or effective date. Pension amount revisions in West Bengal are typically announced through official gazette notifications from the Department of Women & Child Development and Social Welfare, and implemented through the Jai Bangla portal and district-level offices. Until such a notification is confirmed, we recommend treating ₹1,500 as an unconfirmed reported figure rather than a guaranteed new rate.

What Beneficiaries Should Do

  • Continue receiving your current payments as normal. There's no indication that any revision, if it happens, would require beneficiaries to reapply or take any action to continue receiving their pension.
  • Check your bank credit amount directly each month rather than relying on unofficial reports, since your bank statement is the most reliable confirmation of whatever amount is actually being disbursed.
  • Watch for official confirmation through the West Bengal Social Security portal, official gazette notifications, or announcements from your local BDO/SDO office.
  • Be cautious of unofficial claims on social media promising specific dates or amounts without linking to an official source, particularly around pension schemes, where misinformation can circulate quickly.

How Pension Revisions Have Worked Previously

When West Bengal has revised pension amounts under the Jai Bangla umbrella in the past, changes have generally applied uniformly across existing beneficiaries without requiring reapplication, with the new amount simply reflected in the next disbursement cycle following the notification. If a genuine revision to Manabik Pension is confirmed, it would likely follow this same pattern rather than requiring any separate action from current beneficiaries.

We'll Update This Page

As soon as an official notification is available confirming (or denying) this reported revision, we'll update this page and the main Manabik Pension Scheme page with the confirmed details. Bookmark this page or check back periodically if you want to stay current on this specific update.
