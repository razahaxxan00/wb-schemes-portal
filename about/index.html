About West Bengal Schemes Portal

West Bengal Schemes Portal (WB Schemes Portal) is an independent, non-governmental informational platform dedicated to empowering citizens across West Bengal with transparent, accurate, and easy-to-understand guidance on state welfare initiatives.

Who We Are & Site Identity

This portal is published and maintained by an independent editorial team of researcher-writers based in West Bengal. We are a private civic-tech project passionate about public welfare awareness, digital literacy, and civic information access.

Zero Government Affiliation: WB Schemes Portal is strictly an independent information resource. We are NOT affiliated with, operated by, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Government of West Bengal, any state department, or any municipal corporation. For official transactions, official applications, or document downloads, always visit designated official portals such as wb.gov.in.

Our Mission & Public Service

Navigating government welfare schemes can often be challenging due to fragmented portal links, complex eligibility guidelines, and evolving application deadlines. Our mission is to bridge this information gap by providing comprehensive, step-by-step guides for all major state schemes, including Lakshmir Bhandar, Krishak Bandhu, Swasthya Sathi, Kanyashree Prakalpa, Aikyashree, and Banglar Bari.

Editorial Policy & Standards

Every scheme guide published on WB Schemes Portal undergoes rigorous research and multi-stage verification against official government sources:

  • Official Source Research: All information is researched and cross-checked directly against official gazette notifications, government press releases, and departmental portals (such as wb.gov.in, krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in, and swasthyasathi.gov.in).
  • Editorial Review: Before publishing, articles are reviewed for factual clarity and plain-language readability to remove unnecessary bureaucratic jargon.
  • Updates & Corrections: Welfare policies, income thresholds, and official portal links change over time. We review our content regularly. When official updates occur or if a factual error is identified, our editorial team investigates and updates the content promptly.
  • Direct Official Referrals: We provide direct links to official state portals so users can submit official applications and check status securely on official platforms.

Advertising & Monetization Disclosure

To keep this public informational resource 100% free for all readers and cover ongoing server hosting and research expenses, WB Schemes Portal displays third-party advertisements (such as via Google AdSense).

Editorial Independence: Advertisements are strictly separated from our editorial content. Advertisers do not influence our scheme guides, eligibility breakdowns, or rankings. We do not accept payment to promote specific welfare programs or alter eligibility facts.

For detailed information regarding third-party ad networks, cookies, and ad personalization controls, please review our Privacy Policy.
