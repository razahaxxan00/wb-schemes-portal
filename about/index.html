About Us

About West Bengal Schemes Portal

West Bengal Schemes Portal is an independent, non-governmental informational platform created to help citizens across West Bengal easily find, understand, and apply for state welfare programs.

Our Mission

Navigating government welfare schemes can often be confusing due to fragmented websites, complex eligibility criteria, and changing application processes. Our goal is to provide clear, accurate, and up-to-date information on every major West Bengal scheme — including Krishak Bandhu, Swasthya Sathi, Lakshmir Bhandar, Kanyashree Prakalpa, Aikyashree, and more.

What We Provide

  • Step-by-Step Application Guides: Detailed instructions for both online portal registrations and offline camp/BDO application procedures.
  • Status Tracking & Downloads: Guides on checking beneficiary status, searching Gram Panchayat lists, and downloading official cards.
  • Scheme Updates: Timely notifications regarding policy revisions, such as the July 2026 merger and transition of Swasthya Sathi with Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY).
  • Categorized Directory: Organized hubs by beneficiary type (Farmers, Women, Students, Senior Citizens, Minorities, Disability).

Important Disclaimer

Please Note: This portal is an independent informational guide and is NOT an official government portal. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Government of West Bengal or any of its departments.

All scheme names, logos, and trademarks belong to their respective government departments. Citizens are advised to verify all critical eligibility details and official notifications on official government portals (such as krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in, swasthyasathi.gov.in, wbmdfc.org, etc.) before submitting formal applications.
