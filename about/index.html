About West Bengal Schemes Portal

West Bengal Schemes Portal (WB Schemes Portal) is an independent, non-governmental informational platform dedicated to empowering citizens across West Bengal with transparent, accurate, and easy-to-understand guidance on state welfare initiatives.

Our Mission & Public Service

Navigating government welfare schemes can often be challenging due to fragmented portal links, complex eligibility guidelines, and evolving application deadlines. Our mission is to bridge this information gap by providing comprehensive, step-by-step guides for all major state schemes — including Lakshmir Bhandar, Krishak Bandhu, Swasthya Sathi, Kanyashree Prakalpa, Aikyashree, and Banglar Bari.

Editorial Standards & E-E-A-T Commitment

Every scheme guide published on WB Schemes Portal undergoes rigorous research and fact-checking against official gazette notifications, government press releases, and departmental portals (such as wb.gov.in, krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in, and swasthyasathi.gov.in).

  • Fact-Checked Information: Updated regularly to reflect official benefit amounts, income limits, and required document checklists.
  • Step-by-Step Instructions: Clear explanations for online status checking, Gram Panchayat beneficiary list searches, and Duare Sarkar camp procedures.
  • Direct Official Referrals: Direct links to official state portals so users can submit official forms securely.

Important Disclaimer & Transparency Disclosure

Please Note: WB Schemes Portal is an independent private informational guide and is NOT an official government portal. We are not affiliated with, authorized by, or associated with the Government of West Bengal or any municipal corporation/Panchayat office.

We do not collect personal identification documents, issue welfare cards, or charge any fees. All official services must be accessed directly through designated government channels (wb.gov.in) or local Duare Sarkar camps.
