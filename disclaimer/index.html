Official Disclaimer & Legal Notice

Last updated: August 2026 | Public Utility Transparency Statement

⚠️ Non-Governmental Independence Disclosure:

WB Schemes Portal (wb-schemes-portal-three.vercel.app) is an independent, non-governmental public informational initiative created solely for public awareness. We are NOT affiliated with, authorized by, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected to the Government of West Bengal, any municipal corporation, Gram Panchayat, or official state department.

1. Purpose of This Portal

The primary objective of this website is to organize publicly available information regarding West Bengal welfare schemes — including Krishak Bandhu, Swasthya Sathi, Lakshmir Bhandar, Kanyashree, Aikyashree, and Banglar Bari — into clear, accessible step-by-step guides.

2. No Financial Transactions or Application Processing

WB Schemes Portal does NOT process government applications, collect personal identity documents (such as Aadhaar, Voter ID, Ration Card, or Bank details), or collect any fees. No financial transactions or official submissions are conducted through this website.

3. Verification Against Official State Portals

While every effort is made to maintain accurate, up-to-date information based on official state gazettes and department releases, scheme rules, benefit amounts, and eligibility requirements may be modified by government authorities at any time. Users are strongly advised to verify all details directly on official state websites:

  • Official West Bengal Government Portal: wb.gov.in
  • Department of Agriculture (Krishak Bandhu): krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in
  • Health & Family Welfare (Swasthya Sathi): swasthyasathi.gov.in
  • Food & Supplies (Khadya Sathi / Ration): wbpds.wb.gov.in
  • Minorities' Development (Aikyashree): wbmdfc.org

4. Intellectual Property & Trademarks

All scheme titles, government department names, official acronyms, and registered logos mentioned on this website belong to their respective government entities. Their inclusion on this portal is for identification and educational reference only.

5. Contact & Support

If you identify an outdated link, policy revision, or typographical error, please inform our editorial team via our Contact Page or write directly to contact@wb-schemes-portal-three.vercel.app.
