Shikshashree Scheme — Scholarship for SC/ST Students, Class V-VIII

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Shikshashree is a pre-matric scholarship scheme introduced by the Government of West Bengal in 2014-15, run by the Backward Classes Welfare Department, providing annual financial assistance to Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) students studying in Classes V to VIII. It was created by merging two earlier schemes — the Book Grant and the Maintenance Grant — into one simplified programme.

What is Shikshashree Scheme?

Shikshashree aims to reduce dropout among SC/ST students during the critical Class V to VIII years, when the cost of books, stationery, and daily travel can push families to pull children out of school. The scholarship is paid directly to the student's bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

Shikshashree Eligibility Criteria

  • Must belong to a Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) community, with proper certification
  • Must be a permanent resident of West Bengal
  • Must be studying in Class V, VI, VII, or VIII in a government or government-aided/government-recognised school
  • Must be a day scholar — hostellers are not eligible under this scheme (they have separate hostel-based welfare benefits)
  • Annual family income must not exceed ₹2.5 lakh
  • The scholarship is not continued if a student repeats (fails) a class

Shikshashree Scholarship Amount

  • SC students, Class V, VI, VII: ₹750 per year
  • SC students, Class VIII: ₹800 per year
  • ST students, Class V to VIII: a flat ₹800 per year

Amounts are transferred directly to the student's bank account each academic year.

Official OASIS Scholarship Portal Link

Official Portal: oasis.gov.in

How to Apply for Shikshashree Scheme

Shikshashree is an offline, school-based scheme — there's no individual online application:

  1. Contact your school's headmaster/headteacher, who processes registration through the official scholarship portal (OASIS)
  2. The school collects the required documents from eligible students and submits the registration
  3. Required documents typically include: student's Aadhaar card, SC/ST caste certificate, income certificate (family income under ₹2.5 lakh, signed by a competent authority such as the Gram Panchayat Pradhan or Councillor), and a bank passbook (student's or guardian's, Aadhaar-linked)
  4. Once verified, the scholarship amount is credited to the student's bank account
