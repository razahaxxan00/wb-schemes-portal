How to Apply for Bangla Shasya Bima Online

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Farmers can register for Bangla Shasya Bima online each season. This guide covers online registration, downloading the application/claim form, and reporting crop loss.

How to Apply for Bangla Shasya Bima Online

  1. Visit the official Bangla Shasya Bima portal banglashasyabima.net
  2. Log in or register with your basic details, then select the current season (Kharif/Rabi)
  3. Enter personal details, land records, and crop information
  4. Upload required documents — Aadhaar card, bank account details, and land ownership/cultivation proof
  5. Submit the form; loanee farmers are usually enrolled automatically through their bank, while non-loanee farmers complete registration directly on the portal

Bangla Shasya Bima Application Form PDF

The application form for the current season (e.g. "Application Form for Rabi") is available for download directly from the official portal after logging in. Loanee farmers generally don't need to fill a separate form, since their bank submits details on their behalf.

Official Registration & Download Portal

Official Portal: banglashasyabima.net

Reporting Crop Loss / Filing a Claim

  1. Log in to the official portal
  2. Go to the "Report Crop Loss" section
  3. Select your insurance company and season
  4. Follow the on-screen steps to report the loss, or use the toll-free number/email listed for your insurance provider

Remember that compensation is area-based — an individual report contributes to your area's damage assessment, but payout depends on whether the wider Mouza/Panchayat area is declared affected.

Troubleshooting FAQs
