Bangla Shasya Bima — Free Crop Insurance for West Bengal Farmers

Bangla Shasya Bima (BSB) is a crop insurance scheme run by the Government of West Bengal, in partnership with an empanelled insurance company, to protect farmers against crop loss from floods, droughts, pests, and other natural calamities. Farmers pay no premium — the state government bears the full cost on their behalf.

What is Bangla Shasya Bima Scheme?

Bangla Shasya Bima provides financial compensation to farmers whose notified crops are damaged due to natural disasters. It covers both loanee farmers (who've taken an agricultural loan from a registered bank or institution) and non-loanee farmers growing notified crops. Compensation is calculated per hectare and paid directly to the farmer's bank account.

Bangla Shasya Bima Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be a permanent resident of West Bengal
  • Must be actively cultivating one of the notified crops for the season (Kharif/Rabi)
  • Loanee farmers (those with an agricultural loan from a registered bank/institution) are automatically covered
  • Non-loanee farmers growing notified crops can also opt in
  • Applicant should not be a loan defaulter
  • Valid Aadhaar, bank account, and land records are required for registration

How Bangla Shasya Bima Claims Work

Unlike individual-assessment insurance, Bangla Shasya Bima works on an "Area-Based Damage" model:

  • A specific Mouza (village unit) or Panchayat area is officially declared "affected" only when at least 50% or more of farmers in that area suffer crop loss
  • If only a few individual farmers face damage while most of the area's harvest is fine, compensation is not triggered for that area
  • Once an area is declared affected, eligible farmers in that area receive compensation automatically, calculated per hectare, without needing to file an individual claim in most cases
  • Payouts are typically processed in stages across a season

Bangla Shasya Bima Scheme Benefits

  • Zero premium cost to the farmer — fully state-funded
  • Coverage against floods, droughts, pests, and other notified natural calamities
  • Direct bank transfer of compensation, no manual disbursement
  • Covers both loanee and non-loanee farmers
