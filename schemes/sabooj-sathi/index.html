Sabooj Sathi — Free Bicycles for West Bengal Students

Sabooj Sathi (সবুজ সাথী, "Green Companion") is a West Bengal Government scheme, launched in 2015, that provides free bicycles to students of Class 9 to 12 studying in government, government-aided, and government-sponsored schools and madrasahs — helping them commute to school and stay in education.

What is Sabooj Sathi Scheme?

Sabooj Sathi was launched with the tagline "Wheels of Change" to reduce school dropout rates, especially among girl students who often travel long distances to reach school. The West Bengal SC & ST Development & Finance Corporation acts as the nodal agency for procurement and distribution of bicycles. Since launch, over 1.2 crore bicycles have been distributed, and the scheme has received the World Summit on the Information Society (WSIS) award from the United Nations.

Sabooj Sathi Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be a student studying in Class 9, 10, 11, or 12
  • Must be enrolled in a government school, government-aided school, government-sponsored school, or madrasah in West Bengal
  • Students of private schools are not covered under the scheme
  • Eligible student lists are prepared and submitted by the concerned school to the department — students don't apply individually

Sabooj Sathi Scheme Benefits

  • Free bicycle provided to every eligible student, at no cost
  • Reduces travel time and dropout rates, particularly benefiting girl students in rural areas
  • Distribution is coordinated through the student's own school
  • Covers boards across the state — West Bengal Board as well as CBSE/ICSE-affiliated government and aided schools

How Distribution Works

Bicycles are not distributed all at once to every student — the government distributes them in phases based on procurement and stock availability at each school. If you're in an eligible class but haven't received your bicycle yet, it usually means your school's stock is still pending, not that you've been excluded.
