How to Log In to the Sabooj Sathi Portal

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

The Sabooj Sathi portal has two login types — a student login to check your bicycle distribution status, and a school login used by teachers to manage student lists. This guide covers both.

Sabooj Sathi Student Login — Step by Step

  1. Visit the official Sabooj Sathi portal School Administration & District Education Office
  2. Go to the Student Login section
  3. Enter your Applicant ID (also called Student ID) and Date of Birth
  4. Click Login to view your bicycle distribution status

Sabooj Sathi Login with School Code

The "Student ID" requested on the portal is not your regular school roll number — it's your Banglar Shiksha ID, a 14-digit unique number assigned to every student in the Banglar Shiksha system. If you don't know your Banglar Shiksha ID, your school can look it up for you through their own school login on the Banglar Shiksha portal.

Sabooj Sathi School Login (for teachers/schools)

Schools log in separately using their institution credentials to submit and update the list of eligible students for their school, and to track bicycle stock status for distribution.
