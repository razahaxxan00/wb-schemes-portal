Samabyathi Prakalpa — Funeral Assistance for Poor Families

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Samabyathi Prakalpa (সমব্যথী প্রকল্প, "Shared Grief") is a West Bengal Government scheme, launched in 2016, that provides a one-time grant of ₹2,000 to poor families to help cover cremation or burial expenses after the death of a family member.

What is Samabyathi Prakalpa Scheme?

The scheme gives immediate cash assistance — not a bank transfer — to the next of kin of a deceased person at the time of the funeral, so that families under financial stress aren't further burdened while arranging last rites. If no close family member is available, a neighbour who arranges the cremation/burial can apply for the grant instead.

Samabyathi Prakalpa Eligibility Criteria

  • The deceased must have been a permanent resident of West Bengal
  • The scheme is aimed at poor and economically weaker families, specifically those in genuine financial need
  • Cremation or burial must take place at a crematorium, burning ghat, or burial ground within West Bengal
  • The grant is given to the next of kin; if no family member is available, the nearest relative or a neighbour who applies for the Cremation/Burial Certificate can receive it on humanitarian grounds

Samabyathi Prakalpa Amount

A one-time ex-gratia grant of ₹2,000, paid in cash — not transferred to a bank account — so the family can use it immediately at the time of the funeral itself.

How to Apply for Samabyathi Prakalpa

  1. A close relative of the deceased submits a simple application on plain paper to the Block Development Officer (BDO) of the concerned area, or to the officer authorised by the Urban Local Body at the cremation/burial ground
  2. The application must generally be submitted in person, along with proof that the deceased was a permanent West Bengal resident and proof of death/cremation-burial details
  3. The authorised officer examines the application and, once satisfied it's genuine, sanctions and disburses the ₹2,000 grant in cash — either on the spot at the funeral, or shortly after, once the Cremation/Burial Certificate is issued
  4. In rural areas, the grant can also be sanctioned and disbursed through an Extension Officer of the Block or the Secretary of the Gram Panchayat concerned
