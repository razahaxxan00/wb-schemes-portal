OASIS Scholarship — West Bengal's SC/ST/OBC Scholarship Portal

OASIS is a West Bengal Government scholarship portal, run by the Backward Classes Welfare Department, providing financial assistance to Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), and Other Backward Class (OBC) students at both Pre-Matric and Post-Matric levels. Applications, renewals, and status tracking are all handled online through the official OASIS portal.

What is OASIS Scholarship?

OASIS covers a range of scholarship schemes under one portal — Pre-Matric SC, Pre-Matric ST, Pre-Matric OBC, Pre-Matric Component-II, Post-Matric SC, Post-Matric ST, and Post-Matric OBC — so eligible students select the scheme matching their category and class rather than applying to separate systems.

OASIS Scholarship Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be a permanent resident of West Bengal
  • Must belong to SC, ST, or OBC category, with a valid and current caste certificate
  • Pre-Matric level: generally Class 9 and Class 10 students in a recognised school
  • Post-Matric level: Class 11 onwards, including undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional courses in a recognised institution
  • Annual family income limits vary by category — commonly cited around ₹2.5 lakh for SC/ST and a lower limit (around ₹1 lakh) for OBC, so check the exact current limit for your category on the portal
  • ST students must complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) before applying on OASIS

OASIS Scholarship Amount

The scholarship amount varies by education level, category, course type, and whether the student is a day scholar or hosteller (hostellers generally receive a higher amount). Reported annual figures range roughly from ₹1,000 up to ₹13,500 depending on these factors, paid via Direct Benefit Transfer to the student's bank account.

Official OASIS Scholarship Portal Link

Official Portal: oasis.gov.in

How to Apply for OASIS Scholarship

  1. Visit the official OASIS portal (oasis.gov.in) oasis.gov.in
  2. Choose Fresh Registration (new applicant) or Renew Scholarship (returning student), or log in if already registered
  3. Select your district, category, and the applicable scheme (Pre-Matric or Post-Matric)
  4. Fill in personal, academic, and bank account details
  5. Upload required documents — caste certificate, income certificate, previous mark sheet, Aadhaar, and bank passbook
  6. Submit and note your application reference for status tracking
