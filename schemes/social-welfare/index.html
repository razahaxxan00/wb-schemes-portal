West Bengal Social Welfare Schemes — Complete List

The Government of West Bengal runs a wide range of social welfare schemes covering health, pensions, housing, farmer support, and family assistance. This page brings together every major welfare scheme in one place, so you can quickly find the one relevant to you.

Schemes in This Category

Krishak Bandhu

Financial assistance and life insurance for farmers in West Bengal.

Swasthya Sathi

₹5 lakh cashless health insurance cover per family per year.

Lakshmir Bhandar

Monthly financial assistance for women heads of household.

Jai Bangla Pension Scheme

Unified pension scheme for unorganized sector workers and elderly.

Manabik Pension Scheme

Monthly pension for persons with disabilities.

Samabyathi Prakalpa

One-time cash funeral assistance grant for poor families.

Banglar Bari Prakalpa

₹1.20 lakh rural housing construction assistance for EWS families.

Khadya Sathi

Subsidized ration/food security offering rice and wheat at ₹2/kg.
