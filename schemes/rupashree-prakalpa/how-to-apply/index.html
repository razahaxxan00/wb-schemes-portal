Application Guide

How to Apply for Rupashree Prakalpa

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Rupashree Prakalpa applications are submitted through your local Block Development Office (rural) or Sub-Divisional Office (urban), before the marriage date. This guide covers the application steps, form, and documents needed.

How to Apply for Rupashree Prakalpa

  1. Collect the Rupashree Prakalpa application form from your Block Development Office (BDO) — for rural areas — or Sub-Divisional Office (SDO) — for urban/town areas. The form can also be downloaded online.
  2. Fill in all required details — applicant (bride) information, family income, proposed marriage date, and bank account details.
  3. Attach the required documents (see list below) and submit the form at the BDO/SDO office.
  4. The receiving officer verifies your application and hands you a signed, stamped acknowledgement slip/receipt — keep this safely, as it's needed to check your status later.
  5. Once approved, the ₹25,000 grant is transferred through IFMS directly to the bride's bank account.

Rupashree Prakalpa Required Documents

  • Proof of age of bride (18+) and groom (21+) — birth certificate, Madhyamik admit card, or similar
  • Proof of West Bengal residency (5+ years)
  • Aadhaar card
  • Family income certificate
  • Bride's own bank account passbook
  • Proposed marriage date and venue details
  • Passport-size photograph

Application Timing — Important

Submit your application 30 to 60 days before the marriage date. Applications submitted after the wedding has taken place are not accepted, so apply well in advance rather than close to the date.

Troubleshooting FAQs

From your local BDO office (rural) or SDO office (urban), or by downloading it online and printing it before submission.

There's no single centralised online status portal — the fastest way is to visit your BDO/SDO office with your Application Receipt Number, or call the WB Government helpline (1800-345-5505) with your application details.

Applications must be submitted before the marriage takes place — visit your BDO/SDO office as early as possible to avoid missing the window, since post-marriage applications aren't accepted.

Visit your BDO/SDO office in person with your personal details and proposed marriage date — they can look up your application in the district database.
