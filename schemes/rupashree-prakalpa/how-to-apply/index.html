How to Apply for Rupashree Prakalpa — Step by Step (2026)

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wbrupashree.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Completing your rupashree prakalpa apply online or offline submission before your scheduled wedding date ensures timely sanction of the ₹25,000 Direct Benefit Transfer grant.

Timing: Apply Before You Think You Need To

The single most important thing to understand about Rupashree Prakalpa is that timing is strict. Applications must be submitted at least 30 days before the wedding date, with 60 days recommended to allow for processing delays. Critically, applications submitted after the marriage has already taken place are not accepted at all — there's no retroactive route to the grant. If a wedding date is set, applying should be one of the first administrative tasks on the list, not something left until closer to the date.

Documents Checklist

Gather these before visiting your local office or beginning your application process:

  • Proof of the bride's age: Birth certificate, Madhyamik admit card, Voter ID, or Aadhaar card showing age 18+.
  • Proof of West Bengal residence: Voter ID, Aadhaar card, or residence certificate.
  • Family income certificate: Issued by BDO, SDO, or Executive Officer confirming annual family income at or below ₹1.5 lakh.
  • Marriage registration certificate: Or official venue booking slip / invitation card showing registration is planned.
  • Bride's own bank account details: Passbook first page showing account number, IFSC code, and single holder name. The grant goes strictly to the bride's account, not a family member's.
  • Recent passport-size photographs: Self-attested photographs of both the bride and groom.

To check full eligibility details and income thresholds, review our primary overview. For school-age girls, explore Kanyashree Prakalpa grants.

How to Apply, Step by Step

Follow this step-by-step procedure to submit your application correctly:

  1. Confirm your eligibility against the criteria on the main Rupashree Prakalpa page — bride 18+, first marriage, West Bengal resident, family income within ₹1.5 lakh.
  2. Gather all documents from the checklist above.
  3. Visit your local Block Development Officer (BDO) office if you're in a rural area, or the Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) office if you're in an urban/town area.
  4. Collect the official Rupashree Prakalpa application form at the office (or download it from the official portal at wbrupashree.gov.in).
  5. Fill in the form completely and accurately, matching your supporting documents exactly.
  6. Submit the form along with your documents at the counter.
  7. Collect your Application Receipt — this is essential, since it's your main reference for checking status later, and some districts require it to confirm submission.

How to Check Your Application Status

Unlike many other West Bengal schemes, Rupashree Prakalpa generally does not have a single centralised online status-check portal available across every district. The most reliable method is:

  1. Locate your Application Receipt Number from when you originally applied.
  2. Visit the official portal wbrupashree.gov.in and select "Track Status" if supported in your area.
  3. Visit the same BDO or SDO office where you submitted your application.
  4. Provide your receipt number — staff can check your status directly on the district database.
  5. Alternatively, call the West Bengal state helpline (1800-345-5505) for a status update, though an in-person visit with your receipt is generally faster and more specific.

Common Reasons Applications Are Delayed or Rejected

  • Applying too close to the wedding date: Leaving insufficient processing time — this is the single most avoidable cause of a missed grant.
  • Income certificate issues: An outdated or inconsistent income certificate is a common hold-up; make sure yours reflects current, accurate figures.
  • Age documentation gaps: Ensure the bride's proof of age is unambiguous and matches other submitted documents.
  • Bank account details errors: Since the grant goes specifically to the bride's own account, make sure the account is active and details are entered correctly.
  • Missing marriage registration documentation: Confirm with your local office exactly what proof of registration (or planned registration) they require, since this can vary slightly by district.

Field Inquiry and Verification Step-by-Step

Understanding what happens after you submit your application form helps avoid unnecessary delays:

  1. Application Digitization: Office staff at the BDO/SDO counter digitize your paper application and generate an electronic tracking number on wbrupashree.gov.in.
  2. Inquiry Officer Assignment: A local welfare inspector is tasked with conducting a field visit to your declared residential address.
  3. On-Site Verification: The inspector inspects original age proof, speaks with local Panchayat members, and verifies the wedding venue or invitation card.
  4. Sanction Order Issuance: Once approved, the District Magistrate / SDO issues a formal financial sanction order releasing ₹25,000 into the bride's bank account.

Handling Delays and Escalation

If your application remains pending past 30 days without an update, follow these escalation steps:

  • Visit the BDO/SDO office with your Application Receipt slip and request to meet the Rupashree Nodal Officer.
  • Confirm whether your file has been assigned to a field Inquiry Officer or if additional document clarification is required.
  • Ensure your bank account details (IFSC code and account number) are correctly recorded to prevent Direct Benefit Transfer transaction bounces.
  • Call the state social welfare helpline at 1800-345-5505 for administrative tracking assistance.

Bank Account Verification and Aadhaar Seeding

Ensuring your bank account details are correctly configured is essential for receiving the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) grant:

  • Single Account Mandate: The bank account must be held solely in the bride's name. Joint accounts held with parents or spouses are rejected by the treasury portal.
  • Aadhaar Seeding: Verify at your bank branch that your savings account is seeded with Aadhaar and active on the NPCI mapper for government welfare disbursements.
  • Active Account Status: Ensure your bank account has undergone at least one transaction in the past 6 months to prevent dormant status flags.

Special Provisions for Special Needs and Vulnerable Groups

The Department of Women and Child Development provides expedited processing for vulnerable applicant categories:

  • Differently-Abled Brides: Priority field verification is conducted for brides with physical disabilities to ensure rapid grant approval.
  • Orphaned or Destitute Women: Applications supported by certified shelter home superintendents or local welfare officers receive fast-track SDO clearance.
  • Disaster-Affected Families: Special outreach camps organized during Duare Sarkar drives provide on-the-spot document attestation.

Post-Disbursement Audit and Record Archiving

Following successful fund transfer via Direct Benefit Transfer, state audit guidelines mandate final administrative record archiving:

  • Treasury Transaction Clearance: The BDO/SDO office verifies IFMS transaction clearance codes confirming successful credit into the bride's account.
  • Marriage Registration Archive: Beneficiaries are encouraged to submit a copy of their official marriage registration certificate to the local office post-wedding for permanent record closure.
  • Audit Compliance: Annual district social welfare audits review sample application dossiers to maintain administrative transparency and zero-tolerance policy against fraudulent claims.
