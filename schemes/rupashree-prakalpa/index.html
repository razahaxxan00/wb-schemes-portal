Rupashree Prakalpa — West Bengal's Marriage Assistance Scheme

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Rupashree Prakalpa (রূপশ্রী প্রকল্প) is a West Bengal Government scheme, run by the Women and Child Development and Social Welfare Department, that provides a one-time grant of ₹25,000 to economically weaker families at the time of their daughter's marriage. Launched on 1 February 2018, it aims to ease the financial burden of marriage and discourage child marriage by tying eligibility to the legal marriage age.

What is Rupashree Prakalpa Scheme?

Rupashree Prakalpa gives adult daughters from financially disadvantaged West Bengal families a one-time cash grant before their (first) marriage, paid directly into the bride's own bank account — not to the family. The scheme has supported over 12 lakh families since it launched, with a state budget allocation of ₹1,500 crore.

Rupashree Prakalpa Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant (bride) must be a permanent resident of West Bengal, with at least 5 years of residency
  • Bride must be 18 years or older at the time of marriage; groom must be 21 or older
  • Applies only to a first marriage — there's no provision for a second marriage or subsequent children
  • Annual family income should generally not exceed ₹1.5 lakh
  • Application must be submitted before the marriage — typically 30 to 60 days in advance; applications after the marriage has already taken place are not accepted

Rupashree Prakalpa Scheme Benefits

  • One-time grant of ₹25,000, transferred directly to the bride's bank account via IFMS
  • Money is given to the bride herself, not the family or as a cheque
  • Not counted as taxable income for the bride
  • Helps prevent families from taking high-interest loans to cover wedding costs
  • Ties financial support to the legal marriage age, discouraging child marriage

Official Information & Support

Official Helpline: 1800-345-5505

Application Portal / BDO Information: Visit your local BDO (rural) or SDO (urban) office before the wedding date.
