Rupashree Prakalpa — ₹25,000 Marriage Grant for Daughters (2026)

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wbrupashree.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

The Rupashree Prakalpa scheme is West Bengal's dedicated social welfare grant delivering a one-time financial assistance of ₹25,000 directly into the bank accounts of adult women from low-income households prior to their first marriage.

What Is Rupashree Prakalpa

Rupashree Prakalpa is a West Bengal government scheme that provides a one-time financial grant of ₹25,000 to economically weaker families for the marriage of an adult daughter. Launched on 1 February 2018, it's run by the state's Department of Women and Child Development and Social Welfare.

The scheme addresses two problems at once: the financial burden that pushes poor families into high-interest debt to fund a daughter's wedding, and the pressure that financial burden can create toward marrying daughters off before they turn 18. By making the grant conditional on the bride being an adult, the scheme builds in an incentive against underage marriage while still providing real financial relief to the family.

Official portal operations and application form distributions are managed electronically via the state portal: wbrupashree.gov.in (Rupashree Online 3.0 platform).

Who Is Eligible

To qualify for the ₹25,000 Rupashree grant, applicants must fulfill the following mandatory conditions:

  • The bride must be 18 years of age or older at the time of marriage — this aligns with India's legal minimum marriage age for women.
  • This must be the bride's first marriage — Rupashree does not apply to second marriages or provide repeat assistance.
  • The applicant (bride) must be a permanent resident of West Bengal.
  • The family's annual income should not exceed ₹1.5 lakh from all sources.
  • The applicant must not have previously received assistance under this scheme.
  • The marriage must be legally registered, or planned for formal social/religious registration.

Because the grant is strictly once-in-a-lifetime and tied specifically to a first marriage, it's worth applying carefully and accurately the first time — there's no route to a second Rupashree grant later.

Rupashree works alongside Kanyashree Prakalpa to ensure continuous support for girls from secondary schooling through adulthood. View all Women Welfare schemes across West Bengal for a comprehensive overview of financial and social security initiatives.

What You Get

The core benefit package under Rupashree Prakalpa includes:

  • ₹25,000 One-Time Grant: A single payment transferred directly to the bride's personal single bank account — not to her parents, groom, or family head, and never via physical cheques or cash.
  • Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT): Payments are processed through the Integrated Financial Management System (IFMS) directly into the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account prior to or around the wedding date.
  • Tax-Exempt Financial Assistance: The grant is completely non-taxable welfare assistance under state regulations.

It's worth noting that the state occasionally discusses revising the grant amount; as of the most recent confirmed information, the amount remains ₹25,000, but you should always verify the current figure on the official portal before applying, since a pending application at the time of any change is not guaranteed to automatically receive a revised amount.

Documents Required

Prepare clear photocopies and original documents for verification before submitting your application:

  • Age Proof of Bride: Birth certificate, Madhyamik admit card, Voter ID, or Aadhaar card confirming 18+ years.
  • Groom's Age Proof: Birth certificate, Madhyamik admit card, Voter ID, or Aadhaar card confirming 21+ years.
  • Proof of West Bengal Residence: Voter ID, Aadhaar card, or certificate from BDO/Panchayat Pradhan/Municipal Commissioner.
  • Family Income Certificate: Income certificate issued by a competent authority (BDO, SDO, Joint BDO, or Executive Officer) confirming annual income ≤ ₹1.5 Lakh.
  • Bride's Single Bank Account Passbook: First page showing account number, IFSC code, and single account holder name.
  • Marriage Registration / Intention Proof: Marriage card invitation, venue booking receipt, or declaration of intended marriage.
  • Unmarried Declaration: Self-declaration signed by the bride confirming first marriage status.
  • Passport-Size Photographs: Recent colour photographs of both the bride and groom.

When to Apply

Timing matters significantly for this scheme:

  • Applications should be submitted at least 30 days before the wedding date, and 60 days is generally recommended to allow adequate processing time for field verification.
  • Strict Post-Marriage Cut-Off: Late applications, submitted after the marriage has already taken place, are strictly rejected — this is a firm rule with no retroactive exceptions, so planning ahead is essential.
  • Applications must be physically submitted at your local Block Development Officer (BDO) office for rural areas, or the Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) office for urban/municipal areas.

Why This Scheme Exists

In many lower-income West Bengal families, arranging a daughter's marriage has historically meant taking on high-interest debt, selling assets, or spending entire life savings — a burden that can affect a family for years afterward. In some cases, this financial pressure has contributed to families marrying daughters off before the legal age of 18 simply to avoid the cost growing further.

Rupashree Prakalpa was designed to directly address both issues: it eases the immediate financial burden for eligible families, while its 18+ age requirement builds in a structural incentive against underage marriage.

Verification and Field Inquiry Procedure

Following application submission at your local Block Development Office (BDO) or Sub-Divisional Office (SDO), an official field inquiry is conducted before funds are sanctioned:

  • Designated Inquiry Officer: An Inquiry Officer (typically a Block Welfare Officer or Assistant Director of Agriculture staff member) is assigned to verify the physical residence of the applicant.
  • Neighborhood Inquiry: The officer visits the bride's residence to confirm that the marriage is genuine, planned for the declared date, and that the bride is unmarried and at least 18 years of age.
  • Document Cross-Checking: The officer checks original age proof documents and verifies that the family's annual income falls within the ₹1.5 lakh threshold.
  • Approval & IFMS Clearance: Upon satisfactory verification, the BDO/SDO approves the application electronically on the Rupashree Online 3.0 portal, forwarding it to the Treasury for Direct Benefit Transfer.

Role in Preventing Child Marriage

Rupashree Prakalpa plays a crucial role in West Bengal's social reform strategy against early marriage:

  • Financial Incentive: Providing ₹25,000 directly to adult brides incentivizes families to delay marriage until daughters complete age 18.
  • Synergy with Kanyashree: Adolescent girls who receive annual Kanyashree K1/K2 stipends during school transition seamlessly into Rupashree eligibility upon reaching 18 years.
  • Community Awareness: Panchayat Pradhans and Ward Councillors actively raise awareness about the 18+ age mandate during local marriage registration drives.

Financial Assistance Allocation and Treasury Disbursement

The Government of West Bengal allocates dedicated annual budget provisions to ensure uninterrupted disbursement of the ₹25,000 grant:

  • Direct Treasury Transfer: Sanctioned funds are credited directly from the state treasury into the bride's individual bank account via the Integrated Financial Management System (IFMS).
  • No Middlemen: The digital DBT mechanism eliminates intermediaries, ensuring that the full ₹25,000 grant reaches the beneficiary without deductions.
  • Bank Passbook Audit: District social welfare officials audit bank passbooks to verify single account ownership and correct IFSC routing before sanction.
