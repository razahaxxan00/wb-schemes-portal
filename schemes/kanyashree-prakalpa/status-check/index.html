Kanyashree Prakalpa Status Check (2026)

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wbkanyashree.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Track your Kanyashree K1 annual scholarship or K2 one-time grant application status online through the official portal.

How to Check Kanyashree Status Online

  1. Visit the official portal at wbkanyashree.gov.in
  2. Select Track Application Status
  3. Enter your Scheme Year, Scheme Type (K1 / K2), Applicant ID, and Date of Birth
  4. Submit to view whether your application is under verification, sanctioned, or disbursed
