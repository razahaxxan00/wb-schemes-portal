Status Check & Tracking

How to Check Your Kanyashree Prakalpa Status Online

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wbkanyashree.gov.in) · Editorial Review: August 2026

If you've applied for Kanyashree Prakalpa — whether for the K1 annual scholarship or the K2 one-time grant — you can check your application and payment status online in a few minutes through the official Kanyashree Prakalpa portal, run by the Department of Women Development and Social Welfare, Government of West Bengal. This guide walks through the exact steps for checking K1 status, K2 status, what to do if you're moving from K2 to K3, and how to resolve common status-check problems.

Read more →

Why You Might Need to Check Your Status

Kanyashree Prakalpa runs in three connected stages — K1 (annual scholarship for unmarried girls aged 13–18, studying Class VIII–XII), K2 (a one-time ₹25,000 grant paid when a girl turns 18 and remains unmarried and in education), and K3 (ongoing support for postgraduate studies). Because payments and renewals happen every year for K1, and because K2 is a one-time milestone payment, it's common for students and parents to want to confirm exactly where an application stands — especially around admission deadlines, when a delayed payment can create real financial stress.

How to Check Kanyashree Prakalpa Status Online (Step by Step)

  1. Visit the official Kanyashree Prakalpa portal at wbkanyashree.gov.in.
  2. Look for the "Track Application" or "Check Status" option on the homepage — this usually takes you to a dedicated status-check page (commonly found at the URL path kp_track_status.php on the official site).
  3. Select the academic year and the scheme type — K1 (Annual Scholarship) or K2 (One-Time Grant) — from the dropdown menu, since each has its own tracking record.
  4. Enter your Applicant ID / Kanyashree Unique ID and your date of birth exactly as registered in your school records.
  5. Complete the CAPTCHA verification.
  6. Click Submit. Your current status — under verification, approved, sent for payment, or paid — will display immediately on the same page.

Finding Your Applicant ID / Unique ID

Your Kanyashree Unique ID (sometimes referred to as a longer reference number) was generated when your application was first registered, either by you online or by your school on your behalf. It's typically:

  • Printed on the acknowledgement slip given at the time of registration
  • Available from your school's or college's Kanyashree nodal teacher/coordinator, who maintains records for all students registered through that institution
  • Sent to the registered mobile number or email used during application, in many cases

If you genuinely cannot locate it, your school is the fastest route to recovering it, since institutions register and track applications for their own students as a batch.

Checking K1 Status vs K2 Status

K1 and K2 are tracked as separate records on the portal, even though they belong to the same student over time:

  • K1 (Annual Scholarship) status needs to be checked every year you're enrolled in the scheme, since it's a recurring annual payment tied to continued schooling and unmarried status.
  • K2 (One-Time Grant) status is checked once, around the time you turn 18 and apply for the grant. Once K2 is paid, that record doesn't need repeated tracking.

Make sure you're selecting the correct scheme type in the tracking dropdown — a common reason status checks appear to "fail" is simply that the wrong scheme year or type was selected for that student.

Moving from K2 to K3: What Changes for Status Tracking

If you've received your K2 grant and are now continuing into postgraduate or professional studies under K3, tracking works a little differently. K3 benefits are administered in coordination with the Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship (SVMCM) portal, so your Kanyashree ID needs to be linked or transferred as part of your K3/postgraduate registration. If your K3 status isn't showing on the main Kanyashree tracking page, check whether your ID has actually been transferred to the SVMCM system as part of your postgraduate enrolment — this is a common step that gets missed, especially when moving to a new institution for higher studies.

Grievance Status Check

If you've previously raised a complaint or grievance about your Kanyashree application (for example, reporting an error in your registered details or a payment issue), the portal also provides a separate Grievance Status Check page where you can track the resolution status of that specific complaint, separate from your regular application/payment status.

What to Do If Your Payment Is Delayed

If your status shows "approved" but payment hasn't arrived:

  • Confirm your bank account details on file are correct and that the account is still active — a closed or incorrect account is one of the most common reasons a confirmed payment doesn't arrive
  • Check that your Aadhaar and bank account are properly linked, since Kanyashree payments are processed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
  • Confirm with your school's Kanyashree coordinator whether there's a batch-processing delay affecting multiple students at your institution, which is common around the start of an academic year when application volumes are highest

Understanding Status Labels on the Portal

When you check your status, you'll typically see one of a few standard labels. Knowing what each one means saves you from unnecessary follow-up:

  • Submitted / Under Verification — your application has been received and is being checked by your school and/or the district office; no action is needed from you at this stage unless specifically contacted.
  • Approved / Sanctioned — your application has passed verification and has been cleared for payment; funds are typically processed in the next DBT cycle.
  • Payment Processed / Disbursed — the amount has been sent to your linked bank account; if you haven't seen it reflected yet, allow a few working days for it to appear, since bank-side processing can lag slightly behind the portal's own status update.
  • Rejected / Discrepancy Found — there's an issue with your application, commonly a mismatch between your Aadhaar details and school records, an incomplete document, or an eligibility concern (such as marital status). The portal or your school should indicate the specific reason; this needs to be corrected and, in many cases, resubmitted through your institution.

Keeping Your Details Updated for Smooth Status Tracking

A large share of "stuck" or delayed statuses trace back to mismatched or outdated information rather than an actual processing problem. Before assuming there's an issue on the department's end, it's worth confirming:

  • Your name is spelled identically across your Aadhaar card, bank account, and the Kanyashree application — even small differences (a missing middle name, a transliteration difference) can cause automated verification to flag a mismatch.
  • Your mobile number on file is still active, since status updates and OTPs for certain portal actions are sent there.
  • Your bank account hasn't been closed, converted, or changed without updating the linked details on your application.

If you've recently changed schools (moving from a Class VIII–XII institution into a degree college, for instance), make sure your Kanyashree record has been formally transferred to your new institution's nodal teacher — an application left registered under your old school can show as inactive or unresponsive when checked from your new institution's side.
