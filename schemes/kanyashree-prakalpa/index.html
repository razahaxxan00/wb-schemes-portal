Kanyashree Prakalpa — West Bengal's Scholarship for Girl Students

Kanyashree Prakalpa (কন্যাশ্রী প্রকল্প) is a flagship conditional cash-transfer scheme launched by the Government of West Bengal in 2013, run by the Department of Women Development and Social Welfare. It supports adolescent girls from economically weaker families to stay in school, delay marriage until adulthood, and pursue higher education. The scheme received a United Nations Public Service Award for its impact.

What is Kanyashree Prakalpa Scheme?

Kanyashree Prakalpa works in three linked stages — K1, K2, and K3 — that follow a girl student from secondary school through postgraduate study, as long as she stays unmarried and continues her education. Over 76 lakh girls have benefited from the scheme so far.

Kanyashree Prakalpa K1, K2, K3 Details

K1 — Annual Scholarship

  • For unmarried girls aged 13 to 18, studying in Class VIII to XII (or equivalent vocational/technical training) at a recognised institution
  • Annual scholarship, commonly cited at ₹1,000/year, credited directly to the student's bank account
  • Paid every year until the student turns 18, as long as she remains enrolled and unmarried

K2 — One-Time Grant

  • For girls who turn 18 while remaining unmarried and continuing education, higher studies, or vocational training
  • One-time grant of ₹25,000, paid via direct benefit transfer
  • Requires a signed declaration of unmarried status from the student and guardian

K3 — Postgraduate / Higher Education Support

  • For students who completed K2 and are now pursuing postgraduate studies, professional courses, or research degrees
  • Financial aid varies by course, stream, and institution (cited as a monthly stipend or a lump sum depending on the course) — check the current rate on the official portal, as figures are revised periodically
  • Requires passing the undergraduate degree with the minimum marks specified by the scheme

Kanyashree Prakalpa Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an unmarried girl and a resident of West Bengal
  • Family annual income should generally be within ₹1.2 lakh
  • Must be regularly enrolled in a recognised school, college, or training institute
  • A signed unmarried declaration is required at each stage, along with continued enrolment

How to Apply (Summary)

  1. Collect Form K1 (for the annual scholarship) or Form K2 (for the one-time grant) free of cost from your school or college
  2. Fill in personal, bank account, and institutional details, with the required unmarried declaration signed by student and guardian
  3. Attach documents — Aadhaar, birth certificate, bank passbook, previous mark sheet, and passport photo
  4. Submit to your school/college office for verification and forwarding
