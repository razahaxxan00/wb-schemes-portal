Manabik Pension Scheme — Monthly Pension for Persons with Disabilities

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Manabik Pension Scheme (also known as the West Bengal Disability Pension Scheme) is a social welfare scheme launched by the Government of West Bengal on 1 April 2018, run by the Department of Women & Child Development and Social Welfare, providing monthly financial assistance to persons with disabilities from economically weaker families. It now functions as part of the Jai Bangla Pension Scheme umbrella.

What is Manabik Pension Scheme?

The scheme supports disabled residents who are financially weak and unable to earn a stable livelihood due to physical or mental disability, giving them a fixed monthly pension to help manage daily expenses.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be a resident of West Bengal for at least 10 years
  • Must have a certified disability of 40% or more (this was revised down from the original 50% threshold)
  • A valid Disability Certificate issued by a competent authority is required; where unavailable, a medical certificate from a registered practitioner may be accepted
  • Annual family income should not exceed the prescribed ceiling (commonly cited around ₹1 lakh/year)
  • Applicant should not already be receiving another government pension (state or central)

Manabik Pension Amount

Eligible beneficiaries currently receive ₹1,000 per month via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into their bank account. Note: some recent sources cite a revision to ₹1,500/month effective from August 2026 — confirm the current rate on the official West Bengal Social Security portal before publishing, since this figure is subject to government notification.

Official Social Security Portal

Official Portal: jaibangla.wb.gov.in

How to Apply for Manabik Pension Scheme

  1. Collect the Manabik Pension application form free of cost from your BDO office, SDO office, Kolkata Municipal Corporation office, or a Duare Sarkar camp
  2. Fill in your personal, disability, income, and bank account details
  3. Attach required documents — disability certificate (or medical certificate), residency proof, income certificate, Aadhaar card, and self-attested bank passbook copy
  4. Address and submit the completed application to the District Magistrate of your district, along with the prescribed documents
  5. The Block Development Officer or Sub-Divisional Officer verifies the application and conducts an enquiry through an authorised officer — applicants with a terminal illness are given priority in this process
  6. Once sanctioned by the District Magistrate, the pension is transferred monthly to the beneficiary's bank account
