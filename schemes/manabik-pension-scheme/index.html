Manabik Pension Scheme — Monthly Support for Disabled Persons (2026)

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (jaibangla.wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

The Manabik Pension Scheme is West Bengal's monthly financial assistance program dedicated to supporting persons with disabilities (40%+ disability) with a revised monthly pension transferred via Direct Benefit Transfer.

What Is the Manabik Pension Scheme

The Manabik Pension Scheme is a monthly financial assistance programme run by the Government of West Bengal for persons with disabilities from economically weaker families. It was announced in the 2018-19 state budget and came into effect on 1 April 2018, run by the Department of Women and Child Development and Social Welfare. At launch, it absorbed and replaced the state's earlier Disability Pension Scheme of 2010, meaning existing beneficiaries under that older scheme were transferred directly into Manabik.

Today, Manabik operates as one of the sub-schemes under check eligibility for other pension categories via the Jai Bangla Pension Scheme platform, West Bengal's umbrella pension infrastructure bringing together roughly a dozen separate state pension programmes (including old-age, widow, SC/ST, and occupation-based pensions) under a single application form (Form P) and digital disbursement system.

Manabik Pension Amount — Recent Revision

This is the detail most people search for, and it has changed recently, so here's the accurate, up-to-date picture:

  • At Launch (2018): Rs. 1,000 per month.
  • From 1 August 2026: The West Bengal government revised the pension upward to Rs. 1,500 per month for eligible persons with disabilities.

Payments are made through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) straight into the beneficiary's bank account, generally on a monthly cycle. Because many older articles online still quote the original Rs. 1,000 figure, treat that number as outdated unless you're specifically reading about the scheme before August 2026.

Note on eligibility revision: The scheme's disability and income criteria were also revised in 2020. Originally, applicants needed 50% or higher disability and a family income under Rs. 1,00,000 per year. Following the 2020 notification, the disability threshold was lowered to 40% or above, and the income ceiling was reportedly removed. Some sources still list the older 50%/income-capped criteria, so it's worth confirming the current rule with your local BDO or SDO office before assuming you don't qualify.

Eligibility Criteria

Based on the current (post-2020 revision) rules:

  • The applicant must be an Indian citizen and a resident of West Bengal, generally required to have lived in the state for at least 10 years.
  • The applicant must have a disability of 40% or more, certified by a competent medical authority or a valid Disability Certificate / UDID card.
  • The applicant should not already be receiving another government pension or financial assistance scheme covering the same purpose (state or central).
  • There is generally no upper age limit specific to this scheme, though applicants must otherwise meet the eligibility norms set by the department.

Because rules have been revised more than once since 2018, and because Manabik now sits under the wider Jai Bangla umbrella, it's sensible to confirm the exact current criteria with your local BDO, SDO, or Kolkata Municipal Corporation office at the time of applying.

Documents Required

Gather the following documents before submitting your Manabik pension application:

  • Valid Disability Certificate issued by a competent medical authority (or UDID card; a medical certificate from a registered practitioner may be accepted if an official disability certificate isn't available).
  • Aadhaar card.
  • Proof of West Bengal residence (10 years residency proof where applicable, such as Voter ID or Ration Card).
  • Bank passbook copy or account details (showing account number, IFSC code, and single holder name for DBT).
  • Passport-size photograph of the applicant.
  • Age proof (Madhyamik admit card, birth certificate, or Aadhaar).

How to Apply

Manabik Pension applications are processed offline, through local government offices:

  1. Collect the Application Form: Under the Jai Bangla umbrella system, this is the standard Form P, available at your local BDO (Block Development Officer) office, SDO office, Gram Panchayat, or the Kolkata Municipal Corporation counter (as applicable to your area).
  2. Fill in Personal & Scheme Details: Fill in your personal details and select the Manabik / disability pension category on the form.
  3. Attach Required Documents: Attach your Disability Certificate and the other required documents listed above.
  4. Submit at Local Office: Submit the form at the same office and retain any stamped acknowledgment slip given to you.
  5. Verification & Payout: Your application is verified locally before approval by the District Magistrate, after which your pension is added to the monthly DBT disbursement cycle.

Since Manabik is one of several categories under the same Jai Bangla application form, you don't need a separate scheme-specific form beyond selecting the correct category and attaching your disability documentation.

Unique Disability ID (UDID) Card Integration

Under recent digital governance initiatives in West Bengal, the Manabik Pension application process aligns with the central Unique Disability ID (UDID) project:

  • Universal Disability Access: Applicants possessing a valid 18-digit UDID card issued by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment can quote their UDID number directly on Form P.
  • Simplified Verification: UDID card verification speeds up block-level inquiry since disability percentage, medical board approval, and applicant credentials are automatically cross-checked on the Swavlamban portal.
  • Digital Certificate Archiving: Applicants without a UDID card are assisted at local BDO offices and Duare Sarkar camps to enroll for UDID generation alongside their pension registration.

Special Priority Processing and Grievance Redressal

The Department of Women and Child Development and Social Welfare mandates priority field processing for vulnerable applicant categories:

  • Terminal Illness & Severe Disability: Applicants with terminal illnesses or severe locomotor/neurological conditions are prioritized for immediate field verification within 15 days of Form P submission.
  • Duare Sarkar On-the-Spot Registration: During seasonal Duare Sarkar outreach camps, local medical officers conduct physical disability evaluations on-site to issue provisional certificates for pension sanction.
  • Jai Bangla Helpdesk Tracking: Beneficiaries can check their application status or log payment non-receipt grievances by calling the state toll-free helpline at 1800-345-5505 or visiting their local Block Social Welfare Officer.
