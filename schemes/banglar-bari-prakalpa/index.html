Banglar Bari Prakalpa — West Bengal's Rural Housing Scheme

Banglar Bari ("Bengal's House") is a state-funded rural housing scheme run by the Government of West Bengal, providing financial assistance to families living in kutcha (temporary) houses or without any home of their own, so they can build a permanent pucca house.

What is Banglar Bari Scheme?

The West Bengal government launched Banglar Bari as its own state-funded housing programme for the rural poor, delivering grants directly to beneficiaries' bank accounts. In urban areas, the scheme is implemented by the Housing Department; in rural areas, by the Panchayat and Rural Development Department. Beneficiaries are identified through a government survey, after which provisional Eligible, Ineligible, and Inactive lists are published block and gram-panchayat wise for verification.

Banglar Bari Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be a permanent resident of West Bengal
  • Must belong to the Economically Weaker Section (EWS) category
  • Must be currently living in a kutcha (temporary/mud) house, a damaged house, or be without a home of their own
  • Must not already own a pucca house in the name of any family member
  • Selected through a government household survey — families aren't required to apply individually in most cases, but can raise claims/objections if missed from the list

Banglar Bari Scheme Benefits

  • Total assistance of ₹1,20,000 per beneficiary to construct a house, paid in instalments (commonly a first instalment of around ₹60,000, followed by further releases)
  • Higher assistance — around ₹1,30,000 — for beneficiaries in remote areas like Jangalmahal and the Darjeeling Hills
  • Funds transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
  • Aims to replace mud/kutcha houses with durable, permanent structures across the state
