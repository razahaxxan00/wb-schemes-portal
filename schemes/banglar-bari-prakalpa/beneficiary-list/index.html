Beneficiary List Guide

How to Check the Banglar Bari Beneficiary List

The Banglar Bari beneficiary list is published district, block, and gram-panchayat wise, with names sorted into Eligible, Ineligible, and Inactive categories. This guide covers how to check your name online.

How to Check Banglar Bari List Online

  1. Visit your district's official government website, or the West Bengal Housing/Panchayat portal (AwaasSoft beneficiary reports section) prd.wb.gov.in
  2. Look for the "Banglar Bari" or "Rural Housing Scheme" section, usually under Notices or Announcements
  3. Select your Financial Year, District, Block, and Gram Panchayat
  4. Click Submit/View Report to open the list
  5. Use your browser's Find function (Ctrl+F) to search for your name, Aadhaar number, or Registration ID within the list

Official Beneficiary List Portal

Official Portal: prd.wb.gov.in

Understanding List Categories

  • Eligible List — your application has passed verification and you're approved for assistance
  • Ineligible List — your application didn't meet the scheme's criteria (e.g. already owning a pucca house, income above the threshold)
  • Inactive List — your record needs further verification or updated documentation before it can be processed
  • Provisional Wait List (PWL) — you're under consideration but not yet confirmed; a final list follows after the claims/objections window

What to Do If Your Name Is Missing or Marked Ineligible

If you believe you should be on the list but aren't, or you're marked ineligible incorrectly, raise a grievance/objection at your Gram Panchayat office or Block Development Office during the claims and objections window announced with the provisional list — don't wait for the next survey cycle.

Troubleshooting FAQs

Visit your District Magistrate's official website directly — most districts publish block-wise Eligible/Ineligible/Inactive lists as downloadable PDFs under a "Banglar Bari" or "Rural Housing Scheme" section.

Funds are released in phases across large batches of beneficiaries — check for a specific payment date announcement for your phase before assuming there's an issue with your application.
