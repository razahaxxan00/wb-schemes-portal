Beneficiary List & Tracking

Banglar Bari List — Check Your Beneficiary Status (2026)

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Track your Banglar Bari beneficiary list status online by district, block, and Gram Panchayat across West Bengal, and learn how to download official PDF lists and resolve list discrepancies.

Types of Banglar Bari Lists

Before searching for your name, it helps to know that the West Bengal government typically publishes three separate categories of lists for Banglar Bari under each district:

  • Eligible List: Households officially confirmed to receive housing construction assistance.
  • Ineligible List: Households reviewed and found not to meet the scheme's criteria, usually with a stated reason (such as owning an existing pucca house).
  • Inactive List: Entries that are pending further verification, documentation, or administrative action before a final decision is made.

Districts also often publish a Provisional (Wait) List before the final eligible list is confirmed, so a name appearing on a provisional list isn't a final guarantee of assistance until it's carried through to the eligible list.

How to Check the Banglar Bari List Online

Follow these steps to search for your household entry on official state portals:

  1. Visit your district's official website (each West Bengal district maintains its own nic.in portal, e.g., a format like [districtname].gov.in or [districtname].nic.in).
  2. Look for a notice or section titled "Banglar Bari", "Banglar Bari (Gramin) Scheme," or "Provisional/Eligible List of Beneficiaries." These are typically published by the Office of the District Magistrate, Panchayat & Rural Development (P&RD) Section.
  3. Select your Gram Panchayat from the list provided — lists are published Gram Panchayat-wise, not as one combined district file.
  4. Click to view or download the PDF for your Gram Panchayat.
  5. Search the document (using Ctrl+F on a computer, or your phone's PDF search function) for your name, father's/husband's name, or application/survey reference number.

How to Check by District and Gram Panchayat

Because Banglar Bari lists are published separately by each district rather than through one unified statewide portal, the general process looks like this:

  1. Identify your district (for example, Hooghly, Cooch Behar, Malda, South 24 Parganas, etc.).
  2. Visit that district's official government website.
  3. Navigate to the Panchayat and Rural Development / Banglar Bari section, usually listed under "Notices" or "Schemes."
  4. Choose your Sub-Division, then your Block, and finally your Gram Panchayat — most district sites structure the list this way, since Banglar Bari operates at the Gram Panchayat level.
  5. Open the relevant PDF to view the provisional or eligible list for your specific Gram Panchayat.

If you're unsure which district portal to use, your local Block Development Officer (BDO) office or Gram Panchayat office can usually point you to the correct notice or provide a printed copy of the list for your area directly.

What Each List Status Means

Understanding the exact administrative label against your application entry ensures you take the right next steps:

  • Eligible: Your household has been confirmed for housing assistance under Banglar Bari. You can expect further communication about instalment release tied to construction milestones.
  • Ineligible: Your household did not meet the scheme's criteria based on the survey conducted. If you believe this is incorrect (for example, due to a data error), you can raise the issue with your Gram Panchayat or BDO office for review.
  • Inactive: Your entry needs further verification, missing documentation, or administrative confirmation before it can move to either the eligible or ineligible list. This status is not a rejection — it usually just means action is still pending on the record.
  • Provisional/Wait List: Your name is under consideration but hasn't yet been finalised; provisional lists are typically followed by a final eligible list after objections and corrections are processed.

What to Do If Your Name Is Missing

If you believe your household qualifies for Banglar Bari but your name doesn't appear on any of the published lists for your Gram Panchayat:

  1. Visit your Gram Panchayat office and ask whether a fresh survey or inclusion window is currently open in your area.
  2. Check whether your household was previously surveyed under the SECC/BPL housing-deprivation criteria, since Banglar Bari eligibility is largely built on these existing survey records rather than fresh individual applications.
  3. Raise a written request for inclusion at the Gram Panchayat or BDO office, providing your identity, residence, and income documents.
  4. Follow up periodically, since list updates and re-verification rounds are published on an ongoing basis rather than continuously in real time.

Given that rural housing delivery in West Bengal is reportedly being realigned with the central PMAY-G scheme following the 2026 change in state government, it's also worth asking your Gram Panchayat whether a new central-scheme survey affecting your eligibility is underway.

Gram Sabha Approval and Public Vetting Process

The publication of Banglar Bari beneficiary lists follows a strict multi-tier vetting protocol to maintain complete transparency:

  • Gram Sabha Reading: Provisional wait lists are read out loud during open Gram Sabha meetings in each Gram Panchayat to allow community inspection and spot verification.
  • Field Re-Verification by BDO Teams: Multi-member inquiry teams headed by Extension Officers visit households to confirm lack of a pucca house, land ownership, and family income status.
  • District Level Approval Committee (DLAC): Final eligible lists are approved by the DLAC chaired by the District Magistrate prior to publishing PDF notices on district portals.

Step-by-Step Guide to PDF Search on District Portals

Since district housing lists are hosted as large Gram Panchayat-wise PDF documents, follow these search tips to locate your household record quickly:

  • Use Exact Registration / Survey ID: Search using your 18-digit SECC survey ID or application reference number for 100% accurate matching.
  • Search by Head of Family Name: If searching by name, try searching both in English and Bengali spelling variants as transcribed in local Panchayat rolls.
  • Verify Father / Husband Name & Village Code: Always cross-check the listed father's or husband's name and JL number (Jurisdiction List number) to distinguish between similar names within the same Gram Panchayat.

Official Grievance Redressal and Inclusion Appeals

If your eligible household was omitted during field surveys or incorrectly marked under the Ineligible category, submit a formal appeal through these official channels:

  • Written Objection at BDO Office: Submit a written representation addressed to the Block Development Officer along with self-attested photocopies of your Aadhaar, Ration Card, and land Parcha within 15 days of list publication.
  • Duare Sarkar Grievance Desk: Register housing omission complaints at seasonal Duare Sarkar camps for priority field re-inspection.
  • District Housing Cell Toll-Free Helpline: Contact your District Magistrate's office housing cell helpdesk for tracking the status of your submitted objection.

AwaasSoft & Portal Synchronization

Beneficiary list updates and instalment release schedules are synchronized between district NIC web portals and the state AwaasSoft management system. Once your name moves from the Provisional Wait List to the Final Eligible List, your registered mobile number receives automated SMS notifications confirming installment sanction dates and physical house construction inspection milestones.
