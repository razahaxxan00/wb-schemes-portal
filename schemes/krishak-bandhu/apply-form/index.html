Application Guide

How to Apply for Krishak Bandhu Scheme — Online & Offline Process

Applying for the Krishak Bandhu scheme is free and can be done either online through the official portal or offline at your nearest agriculture office. This guide covers both methods, along with the documents you'll need and the application form download.

Documents Required

  • Land records / parcha (Record of Rights)
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Voter ID Card
  • Bank passbook (for Direct Benefit Transfer)
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Mobile number (for OTP verification and updates)

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official Krishak Bandhu portal krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in
  2. Click on "New Registration" / "Apply Online"
  3. Fill in personal details, land details, and bank account information
  4. Upload scanned copies of the required documents
  5. Review and submit the application
  6. Note down your Application/Registration Number for future status checks

How to Apply Offline

  1. Download and print the Krishak Bandhu application form Forms available free at local Krishi / BDO offices
  2. Fill in the form manually with accurate land and bank details
  3. Attach photocopies of required documents
  4. Submit the form at your nearest Krishi office, Gram Panchayat, or government camp
  5. Collect an acknowledgment receipt for tracking

Official Registration & Download Links

Official Online Application Portal: krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in

Application Form PDF: Forms available free of cost at local Krishi Offices and Duare Sarkar camps.

Important Notes

  • Applying for Krishak Bandhu is completely free — never pay anyone claiming to guarantee approval
  • Ensure your land records are updated and match your Aadhaar/Voter ID details to avoid rejection
  • Keep your mobile number active, as updates are often sent via SMS
