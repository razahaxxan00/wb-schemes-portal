Status Tracking Guide

Krishak Bandhu Status Check 2026 — Track Your Application & Payment

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

If you've applied for the Krishak Bandhu scheme, you can easily check your application and payment status online from home — no need to visit any office. This page walks you through the exact steps to check your Krishak Bandhu status using your Voter ID or application/registration number.

How to Check Krishak Bandhu Status Online

  1. Visit the official Krishak Bandhu portal krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in
  2. Click on the "Check Status" or "Applicant Status" option on the homepage
  3. Enter your Voter ID number or Krishak Bandhu Application/Registration Number
  4. Enter the captcha code shown on screen
  5. Click "Submit" to view your current status

What the Status Results Show

  • Application received / under verification
  • Application approved / rejected (with reason, if rejected)
  • Payment status — installment credited or pending
  • Bank account details linked to your application (masked)

Common Issues & Troubleshooting

  • "Record Not Found" — double-check your Voter ID number for typos, or confirm you actually applied
  • Status stuck at "Under Verification" for long — this usually means land record verification is pending at the block level; contact your local Krishi office
  • Payment shown as "Processed" but not received — allow 3–5 working days for bank credit before raising a complaint

Official Status Check Portal Link

Status Check Portal: krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in
