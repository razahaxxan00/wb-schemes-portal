Krishak Bandhu Beneficiary List 2026 — How to Check Online

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

The Krishak Bandhu beneficiary list lets you verify whether your name is officially included among approved beneficiaries. You can check this list online, filtered by your district, block, and Gram Panchayat — here's exactly how.

How to Check the Beneficiary List Online

  1. Visit the official Krishak Bandhu portal krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in
  2. Click on "Beneficiary List" from the main menu
  3. Select your District
  4. Select your Block/Sub-division
  5. Select your Gram Panchayat / Municipal ward
  6. The list of approved beneficiaries in your area will be displayed
  7. Search for your name or Application Number within the list

Official Beneficiary List Portal

Beneficiary List Portal: krishakbandhu.wb.gov.in

Why Your Name Might Not Appear

  • Application still under verification at the block level
  • Land record mismatch between application and official records
  • Application submitted very recently — lists are updated periodically, not in real time
  • Application was rejected — check your status page for the specific reason

What to Do If You're Not Listed

  • Re-check your status individually on the Status Check page first
  • Visit your local Krishi office with your application receipt and documents
  • Request a re-verification if you believe there's an error in land records

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
