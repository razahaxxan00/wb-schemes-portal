Krishak Bandhu Scheme West Bengal — Complete Guide 2026

Krishak Bandhu (কৃষক বন্ধু), meaning "Farmer's Friend," is the West Bengal government's flagship farmer welfare scheme, launched in January 2019 by the Department of Agriculture. It provides direct financial assistance to farmers for cultivation expenses, along with life insurance coverage for farming families. Over 90 lakh farmers across West Bengal currently benefit from the scheme. This page covers everything you need to know — eligibility, benefit amount, application process, and how to check your status.

What is Krishak Bandhu Scheme?

Krishak Bandhu is a Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scheme that offers two types of support to farmers in West Bengal:

  • Annual Financial Assistance — helps cover cultivation costs each crop season
  • Death Benefit Insurance — a lump sum paid to the farmer's family in case of death (natural or accidental) between ages 18–60

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for Krishak Bandhu, an applicant must:

  • Be a permanent resident of West Bengal
  • Be a farmer, sharecropper, or agricultural laborer with recorded land details
  • Own or lease cultivable agricultural land within the state
  • Be between 18 and 60 years of age (for the death benefit component)
  • Hold a valid bank account for Direct Benefit Transfer

Benefits & Amount

  • Farmers owning 1 acre or more of land receive ₹10,000 per year (₹5,000 for Kharif season + ₹5,000 for Rabi season)
  • Farmers owning less than 1 acre receive a proportionate amount, with a minimum support amount fixed by the government
  • In the unfortunate event of a registered farmer's death, the family receives ₹2 lakh as a death benefit
  • All payments are transferred directly to the beneficiary's bank account — no middlemen involved

How to Apply (Summary)

Farmers can apply for Krishak Bandhu either online through the official portal or offline at their nearest Krishi Office / government camp. See full step-by-step guide → Apply / Form page

  1. Visit the official Krishak Bandhu portal or nearest agriculture office
  2. Fill out the application form with land and bank details
  3. Attach required documents (land records, Aadhaar, bank passbook, photo)
  4. Submit the form for verification
  5. Once approved, benefit amount is credited directly to your bank account
