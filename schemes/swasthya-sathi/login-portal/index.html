Swasthya Sathi Login — Beneficiary & Hospital Portal Guide

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

The Swasthya Sathi portal has separate login options for beneficiaries checking their family details and for empanelled hospitals managing patient claims. Here's how each login works.

Beneficiary Login (Mobile Number)

  1. Visit the official Swasthya Sathi portal swasthyasathi.gov.in
  2. Click "Beneficiary Login"
  3. Enter your registered mobile number
  4. Enter the OTP received via SMS
  5. Access your family card details, status, and claim history

Hospital / Empanelment Login

  1. Visit the official Swasthya Sathi portal
  2. Click "Hospital Login" or "Empanelment Login"
  3. Enter your hospital's registered credentials (ID and password)
  4. Access the hospital dashboard for claim submission and patient verification

Official Portal Login Links

Official Login Portal: swasthyasathi.gov.in

Login Troubleshooting

  • OTP not received — confirm your mobile number is correctly registered; try again after a few minutes
  • Forgot hospital login password — use the "Forgot Password" option or contact the Swasthya Sathi help desk
  • Account locked after multiple attempts — wait 30 minutes before retrying, or contact support
