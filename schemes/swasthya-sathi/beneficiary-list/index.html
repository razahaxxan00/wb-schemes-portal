Swasthya Sathi Beneficiary List 2026 — How to Check Online

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Want to confirm your family is officially registered under Swasthya Sathi? You can check the beneficiary list online by selecting your district, block, and ward — here's how.

How to Check the Beneficiary List

  1. Visit the official Swasthya Sathi portal swasthyasathi.gov.in
  2. Click on "Beneficiary List" or "Search Beneficiary"
  3. Select your District, Block/Municipality, and Gram Panchayat/Ward
  4. View the list of registered families in your area
  5. Search for your name, URN, or ration card number within the list

Official Beneficiary Search Portal

Beneficiary Search: swasthyasathi.gov.in

If You're Not Listed

  • Confirm you've completed the full registration process (Form B submission and verification)
  • Recently registered families may take time to appear, as lists are updated periodically
  • Visit your nearest Duare Sarkar camp with your documents if you believe there's an error

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

The list is refreshed periodically as new registrations are verified and processed.

Some district portals allow PDF downloads of ward-wise lists; check availability on the official portal.

Visit your nearest Duare Sarkar camp or Swasthya Sathi help desk with your registration acknowledgment and documents for verification.
