How to Apply for Swasthya Sathi — Complete Registration Guide

Enrolling in Swasthya Sathi is free and can be done online or at a government camp near you. This guide covers the documents you need and the exact steps to complete your registration.

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card (of all family members, if available)
  • Ration Card (used for household identification)
  • Passport-size photograph of the senior-most woman of the household
  • Active mobile number for OTP verification

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official Swasthya Sathi portal or attend your nearest Duare Sarkar camp
  2. Download and fill Form B, or complete the online registration form
  3. Attach photocopies of Aadhaar and ration card
  4. Submit the form at the Health Counter at the camp, or upload documents online
  5. After submission, you'll receive a confirmation SMS
  6. A healthcare agent will contact you for photo and fingerprint verification
  7. Your Swasthya Sathi smart card will be issued after verification

Official Registration & Download Links

Official Registration Portal: swasthyasathi.gov.in

Form B Download: Form B is available free of cost at Duare Sarkar camps, BDO offices, and municipal offices.

Important Notes

  • Registration under Swasthya Sathi is completely free
  • Since West Bengal has joined Ayushman Bharat, new applicants may also want to check eligibility on the Ayushman Bharat portal for nationwide coverage
  • Keep your mobile number active and updated for all future communications
