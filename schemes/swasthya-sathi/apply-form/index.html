How to Apply for Swasthya Sathi

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Swasthya Sathi enrolment isn't fully self-service online, it combines an online data-entry step with in-person verification. This guide walks through the complete application process, required documents, and what to expect at each stage.

Who Can Apply

  • Must be a permanent resident of West Bengal
  • Must not already be enrolled under another government-sponsored health insurance scheme
  • Applicants receiving a medical allowance as part of their salary are not eligible
  • There is no income ceiling, Swasthya Sathi was designed to cover the vast majority of West Bengal households

How to Apply for Swasthya Sathi (Step by Step)

  1. Visit the official portal at swasthyasathi.gov.in
  2. Go to "Apply Online" and select the new enrolment / public data entry option
  3. Fill in your family's details, including all members to be covered (spouse, parents of both spouses, sons up to 18, unmarried daughters up to 21)
  4. Enter biometric and identification details as prompted, some of this is completed at a Duare Sarkar camp rather than fully online, since biometric capture and photographs require in-person processing
  5. Submit your initial data entry and note your reference details for tracking
  6. Attend a Duare Sarkar camp or district enrolment centre for document verification, biometric capture, and photograph collection to complete the process
  7. Once verified, your Swasthya Sathi smart card is issued, typically in the name of the senior-most female member of the family

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar card for all family members being enrolled
  • Proof of West Bengal residency
  • Passport-size photographs of family members
  • Any existing ration card or similar identification, where applicable, for cross-verification

Why the Process Isn't Fully Online

Swasthya Sathi's enrolment intentionally includes an in-person biometric and photograph capture step because the smart card itself stores this data for identity verification at hospitals during treatment. This is different from schemes that only require document upload, since the physical smart card needs to be manufactured and issued with your specific biometric details embedded, which can't be completed purely through a web form.

Tracking Your Application After Submission

Once you've submitted your initial online data entry, check your progress at accounts.swasthyasathi.gov.in under "Check your Application Status" using your application reference. If your status remains unchanged for an extended period after attending your camp verification, following up at your district's Swasthya Sathi office is the fastest way to identify what's pending.

Common Reasons Applications Get Delayed

Incomplete family details — missing a family member's Aadhaar or documentation can hold up the entire household's enrolment, not just that individual's

Biometric capture not completed — if you submitted online data entry but never attended a camp for the in-person verification step, your application remains incomplete regardless of how long ago you filled the form

Document mismatches — inconsistent name spelling or details between Aadhaar and other submitted documents can trigger a manual review, adding processing time

Applying for a Family Member Not Currently Covered

If your family is already enrolled but a new member needs to be added, for example, a newborn child or a newly married family member, this is handled as a correction/addition through Form-based submission at a Duare Sarkar camp, rather than a completely new enrolment application, since it updates an existing family record.

What Happens After You Receive Your Card

Once issued, your Swasthya Sathi smart card is presented at any empanelled hospital for cashless treatment, both government and private facilities within the network are covered. There's no need to reapply annually, coverage continues as long as your registration remains active and your eligibility (permanent residency, no conflicting government scheme enrolment) doesn't change.
