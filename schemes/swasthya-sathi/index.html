Swasthya Sathi Scheme — Coverage, Card & the 2026 Ayushman Bharat Update

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (swasthyasathi.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

The Swasthya Sathi scheme was established to provide cashless secondary and tertiary healthcare coverage of up to ₹5 lakh per family annually across West Bengal. Following state policy revisions in 2026, West Bengal has integrated with the central Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) ecosystem while launching Mukhyamantri Swasthya Bima Yojana (MMSBY) to maintain universal state coverage.

Important Update: What's Changing in 2026

West Bengal's healthcare coverage changed significantly in mid-2026. Following the May 2026 state assembly election, West Bengal's government adopted the central Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) health scheme, officially launching it in the state on 1 July 2026 — West Bengal was the last state in the country to join the nationwide programme. Around 6 crore existing Swasthya Sathi beneficiaries are being migrated into Ayushman Bharat as part of this transition.

This matters directly to you if you hold a Swasthya Sathi card, because the new scheme changes what your coverage can actually do:

  • Pan-India portability — unlike Swasthya Sathi, which only worked at hospitals within West Bengal, Ayushman Bharat coverage works at 36,000+ empanelled hospitals across India, including large referral hospitals outside the state. This is a significant change for the large number of West Bengal families with members working or studying in other states.
  • Coverage amount stays similar — up to ₹5 lakh per family per year, broadly matching what Swasthya Sathi offered.
  • A separate state top-up scheme, Mukhyamantri Swasthya Bima Yojana (MMSBY), launched on 28 July 2026 to cover residents who don't qualify under Ayushman Bharat's central eligibility rules — see Section 5 below.

The rest of this page explains both what Swasthya Sathi originally covered (useful if you're trying to understand your existing card or benefits history) and what to do about the transition right now across all Health Schemes in West Bengal.

What Is the Swasthya Sathi Scheme

Swasthya Sathi was West Bengal's flagship state health insurance and assurance scheme, launched on 30 December 2016. It was designed to give West Bengal families cashless access to hospital treatment — no upfront payment at the hospital counter — for secondary and tertiary medical care, at empanelled government and private hospitals across the state.

The scheme worked on a two-part funding model: coverage up to ₹1.5 lakh was handled through an insurance mode, with the remainder up to the ₹5 lakh annual family limit covered through a state assurance mode. Crucially, the entire premium was paid by the West Bengal state government — beneficiary families never paid anything to be covered.

The scheme was notable for having no family-size limit and covering pre-existing conditions from day one, which set it apart from many private insurance products. Cards were issued in the name of the senior-most woman in the household, and covered her spouse, children, and both sets of parents-in-law under a single family card.

Administered through the State Health Agency under the Department of Health and Family Welfare, the scheme featured a paperless smart card system integrated with pre-authorization software to eliminate out-of-pocket expenses for inpatient admissions.

Who Was Eligible & What It Covered

Under Swasthya Sathi's original operational rules:

  • Eligibility: All West Bengal resident families qualified; there was no income ceiling for enrollment under the scheme's universal expansion announced in November 2020.
  • Documents needed: Typically a Digital Ration Card (used for household identification) and Aadhaar numbers for all family members.
  • Coverage: Up to ₹5,00,000 per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation, including surgical procedures, diagnostic tests, doctor consultations, bed charges, and specialized ICU treatments, plus full coverage for pre-existing diseases.
  • Network: Cashless treatment was available at empanelled hospitals within West Bengal only (historically over 2,800 government and private medical institutions participated).

If you're trying to understand charges from a past hospital stay, or you're checking whether an old claim was processed correctly via the hospital/portal login system, this is the framework those older claims were assessed under.

What Happens to Your Existing Swasthya Sathi Card

If you already hold a Swasthya Sathi smart card, here's what the transition means for you in practical terms:

  1. Your household is expected to be migrated automatically into the Ayushman Bharat database as part of the state-wide rollout — you generally shouldn't need to submit a fresh application from scratch if your ration card and Aadhaar details are up to date.
  2. It's still worth actively checking your eligibility under Ayushman Bharat rather than assuming the migration has already happened for your family — you can check this using your ration card number or Aadhaar number on the official Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY portal.
  3. If your name doesn't appear when you check, this doesn't necessarily mean you've lost coverage — it may mean your household simply hasn't been matched in the underlying eligibility data (SECC 2011) yet. In that situation, keep an eye out for state top-up scheme (MMSBY) notifications, which are specifically meant to catch households in this gap.
  4. For your day-to-day Swasthya Sathi card status, hospital search, or card download in the meantime, see our dedicated guides to check your card status, download your e-card, and see the beneficiary list.

Keep your original physical smart card safely preserved; hospital kiosks continue to reference Swasthya Sathi Unique Registration Numbers (URN) alongside Ayushman Bharat IDs during the administrative transition.

Mukhyamantri Swasthya Bima Yojana — the New State Top-Up

Alongside the shift to Ayushman Bharat, the West Bengal government launched Mukhyamantri Swasthya Bima Yojana (MMSBY) on 28 July 2026. This scheme exists specifically for West Bengal families who do not qualify under Ayushman Bharat's central eligibility criteria, so no household is left without coverage purely because of how the central scheme's eligibility rules are drawn. MMSBY offers coverage broadly in line with the ₹5 lakh per family annual benefit that Swasthya Sathi families were used to, fully funded at the state level.

If you've checked your Ayushman Bharat eligibility and found you don't qualify, MMSBY is the scheme to look out for next — check with your local health department, Block Development Office (BDO), or Common Service Centre (CSC) to apply or update your registration details under this newer state scheme.

Hospital Network & Where You Can Still Get Treated

During the transition period, it's worth understanding how both medical networks function side by side:

  • Hospitals that were empanelled under Swasthya Sathi generally continue to serve patients, and many are also becoming empanelled under Ayushman Bharat as part of the ongoing state rollout.
  • Once your household is confirmed under Ayushman Bharat, you gain access to the much larger national network of 36,000+ empanelled hospitals, including major multi-specialty centres outside West Bengal such as AIIMS Delhi, AIIMS Kalyani, and CMC Vellore.
  • If you're not sure which scheme a specific hospital currently recognises, confirming directly with the hospital's Swasthya Sathi / Ayushman Bharat help desk counter prior to admission is the safest approach during this changeover period.

Emergency admissions remain protected under state healthcare guidelines, requiring empanelled hospitals to provide immediate stabilizing care without demanding advance cash deposits.

What You Should Do Next

  1. Check your Ayushman Bharat eligibility using your ration card or Aadhaar number on the official PM-JAY portal (pmjay.gov.in) or via a nearby CSC.
  2. Keep your existing Swasthya Sathi card — don't discard it, since it remains useful for status checks, historical claims, and as household identification during the transition period.
  3. If you're not found in the Ayushman Bharat database, watch for a state top-up (MMSBY) notification rather than assuming you've lost health coverage.
  4. For eKYC or face-authentication issues while confirming your Ayushman Bharat enrollment, the Ayushman mobile application supports Face Authentication as a convenient alternative, or visit a CSC centre for biometric verification.

If you need to update household member names or address details on official records, download the appropriate modification form from the swasthyasathi.gov.in portal or visit your nearest Duare Sarkar camp.

Frequently Asked Questions

West Bengal formally adopted the central Ayushman Bharat scheme on 1 July 2026, and existing Swasthya Sathi beneficiaries are being migrated into it. Swasthya Sathi itself is being phased out as the state's primary health coverage scheme, though your existing card and its history remain relevant during this transition.

Migration is generally automatic for existing beneficiaries, but it's worth actively checking your eligibility on the official Ayushman Bharat portal rather than assuming it's already complete for your household.

The state's Mukhyamantri Swasthya Bima Yojana (MMSBY), launched 28 July 2026, is specifically meant to cover families who fall outside Ayushman Bharat's central eligibility criteria.

Many hospitals that were empanelled under Swasthya Sathi continue to operate and are being brought into the Ayushman Bharat network — but confirm directly with the hospital before admission during this transition period, since not every hospital's status has necessarily updated yet.

Ayushman Bharat and MMSBY both broadly maintain coverage up to ₹5 lakh per family per year, similar to what Swasthya Sathi previously offered — the main change is that Ayushman Bharat coverage now works across India, not just within West Bengal.

Visit the official PM-JAY portal (pmjay.gov.in) and search using your ration card number or Aadhaar number, or visit your nearest CSC (Common Service Centre) for a manual database check.

Your nearest Duare Sarkar camp, CSC centre, or the local Health & Family Welfare department office can help clarify your current coverage status during this transition.
