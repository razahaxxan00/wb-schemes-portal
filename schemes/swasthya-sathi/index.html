Swasthya Sathi Scheme West Bengal — Complete Guide 2026

Swasthya Sathi (স্বাস্থ্যসাথী) is West Bengal's flagship health insurance scheme, launched in 2016, providing cashless treatment coverage of ₹5 lakh per family per year at government and empanelled private hospitals. Over 2.25 crore families across the state are currently enrolled. This page covers everything about the scheme — eligibility, benefits, card details, application process, and an important update on its integration with the central Ayushman Bharat scheme.

📢 Important Update (July 2026)

Since July 1, 2026, West Bengal has joined the central Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) scheme. Existing Swasthya Sathi beneficiaries are being gradually migrated to Ayushman Bharat, which offers nationwide hospital portability (not just within West Bengal). Swasthya Sathi continues to operate during this transition period and has not been discontinued. Beneficiaries are advised to check both their Swasthya Sathi and Ayushman Bharat card status, and watch for official updates from the state health department.

What is Swasthya Sathi?

Swasthya Sathi is a Direct Benefit health insurance scheme offering:

  • Cashless hospitalization coverage up to ₹5 lakh per family per year
  • Coverage for pre-existing conditions from day one, with no waiting period for most packages
  • Access to 2,800+ empanelled government and private hospitals across West Bengal
  • The card is issued in the name of the senior-most woman in the household

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be a permanent resident of West Bengal
  • All families are eligible — there is no income ceiling for enrollment
  • Must have a ration card (used for household identification)
  • Households not already covered under schemes like CGHS or ECHS

What's Covered

  • Inpatient (hospital admission) treatment, surgeries, and procedures
  • Diagnostics, medicines, and consumables during hospital stay
  • Note: OPD (outpatient) visits and take-home medicines are NOT covered

How to Apply (Summary)

  1. Visit the official Swasthya Sathi portal or attend a nearby Duare Sarkar camp
  2. Submit Form B with required documents (Aadhaar, ration card, family details)
  3. Complete photo and fingerprint verification when contacted by a healthcare agent
  4. Receive your Swasthya Sathi smart card
