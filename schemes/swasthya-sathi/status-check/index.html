Status Tracking

Swasthya Sathi Status Check 2026 — Track Application & Claims

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Whether you want to check if your Swasthya Sathi application has been approved, verify your card status, or track a hospital claim, you can do it all online. This guide shows you exactly how.

How to Check Swasthya Sathi Status Online

  1. Visit the official Swasthya Sathi portal swasthyasathi.gov.in
  2. Click on "Check Status" or "Applicant Status"
  3. Enter your URN, Aadhaar number, or registered mobile number
  4. Enter the captcha and submit
  5. View your enrollment status, card status, or claim status

Official Status Check Link

Status Portal: swasthyasathi.gov.in

What the Status Shows

  • Application under process / approved / card issued
  • Claim status for a recent hospital treatment (approved, under review, or settled amount)
  • Family member details linked to the card

Common Issues

  • "No Record Found" — confirm you've completed Form B registration; check for typos in your URN/Aadhaar
  • Claim status stuck at "Under Review" — hospital-submitted claims can take a few days for processing; contact the hospital's Swasthya Sathi desk for updates
  • Card shows "Inactive" — this may relate to the ongoing Ayushman Bharat transition; visit your nearest Duare Sarkar camp to confirm your active status
