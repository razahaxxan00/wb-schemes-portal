Swasthya Sathi Card Download 2026 — Complete Guide

If you're already enrolled under Swasthya Sathi, you can download your e-card online anytime — no need to wait for a physical card or visit a government office. Here's the step-by-step process, plus what to do if your card is lost or damaged.

How to Download Your Swasthya Sathi E-Card

  1. Visit the official Swasthya Sathi portal swasthyasathi.gov.in
  2. Click on "Download Card" or "e-Card" option
  3. Enter your registered mobile number or URN (Unique Reference Number)
  4. Verify using the OTP sent to your registered mobile number
  5. Your e-card will be displayed — click "Download" or "Print"

Official E-Card Download Portal

Download Portal: swasthyasathi.gov.in

What If You've Lost Your Physical Card?

  • The downloaded e-card is valid for hospital treatment, so you don't necessarily need a physical replacement
  • For a physical duplicate card, visit your nearest Duare Sarkar camp or Swasthya Sathi help desk with your Aadhaar and URN
  • Report a lost card to avoid misuse, especially if it may have been stolen

Troubleshooting

  • "URN Not Found" — double-check the number for typos, or confirm your enrollment was completed
  • OTP not received — ensure your mobile number is updated in the Swasthya Sathi database; visit a Duare Sarkar camp to update it if needed
