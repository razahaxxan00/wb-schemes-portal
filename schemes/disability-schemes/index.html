Disability Welfare Schemes in West Bengal (2026)

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Complete guide to West Bengal government programmes for persons with disabilities (PwD) — covering Manabik pension, free assistive devices, educational scholarships, self-employment grants, and 4% job reservations.

Overview: Support for Persons with Disabilities in West Bengal

West Bengal runs a combination of state and centrally-sponsored programmes for persons with disabilities (PwD), covering monthly financial support, free assistive devices, education assistance, self-employment help, and workplace protections. Most of these are administered through the state's Department of Women & Child Development and Social Welfare, alongside the Office of the Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities, West Bengal.

Nearly every benefit on this page requires you to first hold a valid disability certificate, issued by a competent medical authority confirming your disability type and percentage — so if you don't already have one, that's generally the first step before applying to any of the specific schemes below. Manabik Pension Scheme — the Flagship Programme

The Manabik Pension Scheme is West Bengal's primary monthly financial assistance programme for persons with disabilities, launched on 1 April 2018 and now operating as part of the state's broader Jai Bangla pension umbrella.

  • Monthly Pension: Rs. 1,000 per month (revised to Rs. 1,500 per month effective 1 August 2026 under recent state notifications).
  • Disability Threshold: 40% or higher certified disability (lowered from an earlier 50% threshold in a 2020 notification).
  • Family Income Ceiling: Generally around Rs. 1 lakh per year.
  • Residency Requirement: Applicant must have lived in West Bengal for at least 10 years.
  • Documents Needed: Disability certificate, Aadhaar card, digital ration card or EPIC (Voter ID) card, bank passbook, and a passport-size photograph.
  • Where to Apply: At a Duare Sarkar camp, your local BDO office (rural areas), SDO office (urban areas), or the Office of the Controller of Vagrancy for Kolkata Municipal Corporation areas.

Applicants already receiving another government pension for the same purpose are generally not eligible to also receive Manabik.

Free Assistive Devices (ADIP Scheme)

The Assistance to Disabled Persons for Purchase/Fitting of Aids/Appliances (ADIP) Scheme is a centrally-sponsored programme, implemented in West Bengal through the Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities (Kolkata Municipal Corporation area) and District Social Welfare Offices (for districts), providing free or subsidised assistive devices:

  • Devices Covered: Wheelchairs, tricycles, hearing aids, sewing machines, Daisy Book players and talking devices, digital magnifiers, and similar aids tailored to physical, visual, hearing, or multiple disabilities.
  • Eligibility Ceiling: Any Indian citizen with a disability, whose monthly income (or a dependent's parent/guardian's income, if applicable) does not exceed Rs. 20,000.
  • Reapplication Gap Rule: Beneficiaries generally cannot reapply for the same purpose within 3 years (1 year for children under 12), ensuring the scheme reaches as many people as possible rather than repeatedly serving the same individuals.
  • Distribution Camps: Distribution happens through periodic camps organised by the implementing agency — check with your District Social Welfare Office or the Commissioner's office for upcoming camp schedules in your area.

Economic Rehabilitation / Self-Employment Assistance

For adults with disabilities from low-income families looking to become self-reliant, the state's Women & Child Development and Social Welfare Department provides economic assistance of up to Rs. 10,000 to support self-employment ventures — a one-time grant intended to help cover startup costs for a small trade or business.

Education Support for Students with Disabilities

Targeted educational grants ensuring school retention for children with special needs:

  • The Scheme of Scholarship for Students with Disabilities studying below Class IX (2010) provides financial support to help younger students with disabilities stay in school.
  • Students with disabilities are also supported through the Integrated Education for Disabled Children (IEDC) scheme, which provides book grants, uniforms, and other assistance to help integrate students with disabilities into mainstream schools.

Job Reservation & Workplace Benefits

Statutory workplace rights and recruitment age extensions under West Bengal state policy:

  • Persons with disabilities are entitled to a 4% reservation in government job vacancies in West Bengal.
  • Physically handicapped persons receive an age concession of up to 45 years at the time of entry into government service, extending eligibility well beyond the standard age limits applied to other applicants.
  • A life-long family pension is available to the disabled dependent of a deceased government employee or teaching/non-teaching staff member who cannot earn a livelihood independently.

Travel Concessions & Other Benefits

Mobility subsidies and land allotment preferences for certified PwD residents:

  • Free travel on buses run by State Transport Corporations (excluding Deluxe/Rocket and inter-state services) and on buses and trams run by Kolkata Tramways Company.
  • Railway concessions are available for persons with disabilities (excluding certain premium train categories like Rajdhani).
  • Preferential allotment of land at concessional rates is available to persons with disabilities through the Department of Land & Land Reforms.

Getting Your Disability Certificate First

Since almost every benefit listed above depends on holding a valid disability certificate, this is worth treating as the essential first step rather than an afterthought. The certificate is issued by a competent medical authority (via District CMOH boards or the national UDID portal at swavlambancard.gov.in) and confirms both your disability type and percentage — the specific percentage recorded directly determines which schemes you become eligible for (for example, Manabik's threshold, or ADIP's minimum 40% disability requirement for certain devices). If you don't yet have a certificate, start there before applying to any individual scheme on this page.

Unique Disability ID (UDID) Card Online Registration Workflow

Under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016, West Bengal has migrated to the national UDID portal (swavlambancard.gov.in) for digital certification:

  • Single Digital Identity across India: The smart UDID card eliminates the need for holding multiple paper disability certificates across state and central government departments.
  • Hospital Board Assessment Scheduling: Applicants submit online registration with Aadhaar details, after which an automated SMS notifies them of their physical assessment date at their designated District CMOH Hospital Board.
  • QR-Coded Smart Card Issuance: Once approved by the medical board, a laminated QR-coded UDID card is delivered directly to the beneficiary's home address via registered post.

Accessible Public Infrastructure and Barrier-Free Accessibility Mandates

West Bengal enforces statutory accessibility standards across public transport, government offices, and educational institutions:

  • Ramp and Lift Retrofitting in Public Buildings: Municipalities and district collectorates mandate tactile paving, wheelchair ramps, and braille signage across all public service centers.
  • Accessible Mass Rapid Transit: Kolkata Metro Rail and state bus terminals incorporate audio-visual announcements and dedicated low-floor bus services for PwD commuters.

Ensuring universal accessibility, monthly income pensions, and equal employment opportunities empowers persons with disabilities to live with dignity and independence across West Bengal.
