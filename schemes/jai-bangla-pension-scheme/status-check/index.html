Status & Payment Tracking

How to Check Your Jai Bangla Pension Status

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Whether you're waiting on a new pension application or want to confirm your monthly payment, this guide covers the complete process for checking your Jai Bangla Pension status online, plus what to do if your pension hasn't arrived.

Read more →

How to Check Jai Bangla Pension Status Online

  1. Visit the official Jai Bangla Pension portal jaibangla.wb.gov.in
  2. Click on "Track Application Status"
  3. Select your sub-scheme, old-age pension, widow pension, disability (Manabik) pension, or one of the occupation-based pensions
  4. Enter your Beneficiary ID, Aadhaar number, or registered mobile number
  5. Submit to view your current status, under verification, approved, or payment released

Finding Your Beneficiary ID

Your Beneficiary ID or application number is issued when your Form P application is first accepted at the BDO, SDO, or Municipal office. If you don't have it recorded, your registered mobile number or Aadhaar number can usually be used instead to look up your record on the portal directly.

Understanding Jai Bangla's Sub-Schemes

Jai Bangla is an umbrella system covering 11 distinct sub-schemes, old-age pension, widow pension, disability (Manabik) pension, and occupation-based pensions for farmers, fishermen, artisans, textile workers, purohits, MSME workers, and folk artists (Lok Prasar Prakalpa). When checking status, selecting the correct sub-scheme matters, a common reason a valid Beneficiary ID appears to show "no record" is simply that the wrong sub-scheme was selected in the dropdown.

Payment Timing

Jai Bangla pensions are generally released around the 1st of every month through the West Bengal Integrated Financial Management System (WBIFMS). If you're checking status a day or two after the 1st and don't yet see a credit, this is often simply normal processing time across the large beneficiary base, rather than an individual issue.

What to Check If Your Pension Hasn't Arrived

If your status shows approved but the pension hasn't been credited:

  • Confirm your bank account is properly Aadhaar-linked. DBT payments fail silently if this linkage is broken, showing as approved on the portal while never actually reaching your account.
  • Check for a name mismatch between your Aadhaar, bank account, and pension application, even a small spelling difference can cause an automated transfer to fail.
  • Confirm you're not receiving another conflicting government pension. Jai Bangla generally requires that beneficiaries aren't simultaneously receiving another state or central pension; if this changed after your approval, it could affect ongoing payments.
  • For occupation-based pensions specifically, confirm your underlying occupational registration (such as fisherman or artisan registration) is still valid and hasn't lapsed, since these sub-schemes depend on that registration remaining active.

If Your Pension Stopped After Being Regular

A pension that was arriving reliably for months and then stops is usually linked to one of a few specific causes: a periodic re-verification exercise flagged your record for review, your bank account changed or was closed without updating your registration, or a change in your eligibility status (such as newly qualifying for a different government pension). Visiting your local BDO, SDO, or Municipal Corporation office with your documents is the fastest way to identify which of these applies to your specific case, rather than continuing to check the portal repeatedly without resolution.

Getting Help In Person

If your status check online doesn't resolve your question, or your status has been stuck at "under verification" for an unusually long time, visiting your nearest Duare Sarkar camp or your BDO/SDO/Municipal Corporation office directly, with your Beneficiary ID, Aadhaar card, and bank passbook, is the most reliable next step. Staff there can access more detailed processing information than what's shown on the public-facing status page.

Checking Status for a Family Member

If you're checking status on behalf of an elderly parent or family member who isn't comfortable using the portal themselves, you can use their Beneficiary ID, Aadhaar number, or registered mobile number on their behalf, the portal doesn't require the beneficiary to personally operate the search.
