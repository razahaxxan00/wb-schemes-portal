Status & Payment Tracking

How to Check Your Jai Bangla Pension Status

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

You can check your Jai Bangla Pension application or payment status online in a few minutes using your Beneficiary ID, Aadhaar number, or registered mobile number. This guide covers checking status online, tracking by application number, and what to do if your pension hasn't been received.

How to Check Jai Bangla Pension Status Online

  1. Visit the official Jai Bangla Pension portal jaibangla.wb.gov.in
  2. Click on "Track Application Status"
  3. Select your sub-scheme (old-age, widow, disability, or occupation-based)
  4. Enter your Beneficiary ID, Aadhaar number, or registered mobile number
  5. Submit to view your current status — under verification, approved, or payment released

Official Status Tracking Portal

Tracking Link: jaibangla.wb.gov.in

Checking Status by Application Number

Your Beneficiary ID or application number is issued when your Form P application is first accepted at the BDO/SDO/Municipal office. If you don't have it noted down, your registered mobile number or Aadhaar number can usually be used instead to look up your record on the portal.

Jai Bangla Pension Not Received — What to Check

If your status shows approved but the pension hasn't arrived, check these common causes:

  • Bank account is not properly linked to Aadhaar/DBT
  • Name mismatch between Aadhaar, bank account, and application
  • Payment cycle timing — pensions are generally released around the 1st of the month, so a short delay after that date is common before it counts as genuinely missed
  • Sub-scheme-specific verification is still pending (e.g. occupational registration not yet confirmed)

Troubleshooting FAQs

Confirm your bank account is Aadhaar-linked and check a few days after the 1st of the month, since that's the usual payment date. If it's still missing after that, visit your BDO/SDO office.
