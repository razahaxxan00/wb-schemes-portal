Jai Bangla Pension Scheme — West Bengal's Unified Pension Programme

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Jai Bangla Pension Scheme (also written Joy Bangla) is a West Bengal Government umbrella pension initiative, launched on 1 April 2020, that brings together old-age, widow, disability, SC/ST, and several occupation-based pension programmes under one system, run by the Department of Women & Child Development and Social Welfare.

What is Jai Bangla Pension Scheme?

Instead of administering separate pension schemes through different departments, Jai Bangla consolidates them into a single framework with one beneficiary database, one application form, and one payment system. It currently covers 11 distinct sub-schemes — including old-age pension, widow pension, disability pension, and pensions for farmers, fishermen, artisans, textile workers, purohits, MSME workers, and folk artists (Lok Prasar Prakalpa) — each serving a different group at a common monthly rate.

Jai Bangla Pension Eligibility Criteria

General conditions (exact requirements vary slightly by sub-scheme):

  • Must be a permanent resident of West Bengal
  • Old-age pension: senior citizens above 60 years
  • Widow pension: widows aged 18 years or above, generally from BPL households, with the husband's death certificate as supporting document
  • Disability pension: persons with disabilities, age limit around 55 years
  • Occupation-based pensions (farmer, fisherman, artisan, textile worker, purohit, MSME worker, Lok Prasar Prakalpa): relevant occupational registration document issued by the competent authority
  • Should not already be receiving another government pension

Jai Bangla Pension Scheme Amount

Most Jai Bangla sub-schemes pay ₹1,000 per month (revised to ₹1,500 per month effective 1 August 2026 under the 2026-27 state budget), regardless of category, caste, or district, credited directly to the beneficiary's bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Payments are generally released on or around the first of every month through the West Bengal Integrated Financial Management System (WBIFMS).

How to Apply (Summary)

  1. Collect Form P — the single offline application form covering all Jai Bangla sub-schemes — free of cost from your BDO, SDO, or Municipal Corporation office, or download it from the official portal
  2. Select your relevant sub-scheme from the checklist on the form
  3. Attach required documents: Aadhaar card, West Bengal residence proof, bank passbook (individual account), BPL certificate, two photographs, plus any category-specific document (e.g. death certificate for widow pension, occupational registration for occupation-based pensions)
  4. Submit at your local office or a Duare Sarkar camp

Official Portal: jaibangla.wb.gov.in
