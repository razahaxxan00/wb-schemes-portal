Utkarsh Bangla — Free Skill Development Scheme (2026)

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (pbssd.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Utkarsh Bangla is West Bengal's award-winning skill development initiative delivering free, placement-linked vocational training to youth and job seekers across 23 districts under the Department of Technical Education, Training & Skill Development.

What Is the Utkarsh Bangla Scheme

Utkarsh Bangla is a flagship skill development scheme launched by the Government of West Bengal on 16 February 2016, run under the Department of Technical Education, Training & Skill Development (TET&SD) through the Paschim Banga Society for Skill Development (PBSSD). It forms the core of the state's broader West Bengal Skill Development Mission (WBSDM), and provides placement-linked, short-term vocational skill training to youth and unemployed individuals across the state, entirely free of cost.

The scheme has received international recognition, winning the World Summit on the Information Society (WSIS) award under the United Nations in the Capacity Building category — out of 1,062 nominations across 18 categories, Utkarsh Bangla emerged as the top winner in its category, one of the strongest external endorsements of its design and reach.

As part of West Bengal government schemes, Utkarsh Bangla bridges the gap between formal education and industry demand, ensuring job-ready skills for youth.

Objectives of the Scheme

The primary socio-economic goals of Utkarsh Bangla include:

  • Skill for Sustainable Livelihoods: Skill individuals for wage or self-employment, improving livelihoods through increased earnings, better working conditions, or a move from informal to formal employment.
  • Large-Scale Youth Reach: Reach a large annual target of trainees — the scheme has previously targeted skilling around 6 lakh youths per year across polytechnics, ITIs, and vocational centers.
  • National Standards Alignment: Align training with national standards, with skill modules compliant with the National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF), NCVT, and Qualification Pack–National Occupational Standards (QP-NOS), so certificates carry recognition beyond West Bengal.
  • Inclusion of School Dropouts: Provide vocational training to school dropouts and unemployed youth, helping them re-enter the workforce even without completing formal schooling.
  • Kanyashree Career Integration: Support career-linked technical education for Kanyashree students, since Class 11 and 12 students enrolled under the Kanyashree Prakalpa scheme can access career-centric technical training under Utkarsh Bangla, along with a daily stipend during the course.

Sectors & Courses Covered

Utkarsh Bangla offers training across a wide range of industries, including Agriculture, Apparel, Automotive, Banking & Financial Services, Construction, Electronics, Healthcare, IT-ITeS, Retail, and Tourism & Hospitality, among others. Training is delivered through three main modes under PBSSD:

  • Non-Project Mode: Candidates register directly with PBSSD and are allocated a training provider based on their preferred location and course.
  • Project Mode: Training providers are assigned an annual target and independently mobilise candidates to meet it, including in remote or previously uncovered areas.
  • Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL): Aimed at people who already have practical experience in a trade but lack formal certification, helping them get certified based on their existing skills.

Course duration varies considerably depending on the trade, generally ranging from around 100 to 1,500 hours (some listings cite a 12-hour to 720-hour range for shorter modules), so the time commitment depends heavily on which course a candidate selects.

Benefits of Utkarsh Bangla

  • 100% Free Vocational Training: Completely free vocational training, removing cost as a barrier to skill-building for unemployed or underqualified youth.
  • Placement Support & Industry Drives: Placement support after certification, connecting trained candidates with wage or self-employment opportunities. Industry partnerships include major recruiters such as Samsung, Raymond, Berger Paints, Maruti Suzuki, ASSOCHAM (gems and jewellery), and CREDAI.
  • Integrated Employment Platforms: Access to linked employment platforms, including the Rojgar Seva Portal, the West Bengal Apprenticeship Scheme, the Bhavishyat Credit Card, and loan facilities under Minority Affairs, DSWO, and MSME&T departments.
  • Nationally Recognised Certification: Nationally recognised certification, since training follows NSQF-aligned, Sector Skill Council-defined curricula.
  • Daily Stipend for Kanyashree Trainees: Stipend support for Kanyashree students undertaking career-centric technical training (Rs. 50 per day during the course).
  • Biometric Quality Assurance: Technology-driven, accountable training delivery, using geo-tagged, time-stamped biometric attendance at training centres to ensure genuine participation.

Eligibility Criteria

To enroll in Utkarsh Bangla courses, candidates must satisfy the following general requirements:

  • The candidate must be a resident of West Bengal.
  • The candidate must be at least 18 years of age under general eligibility norms.
  • Specific eligibility (educational qualification, minimum age, or entry requirement) can vary by course — for example, some trades require a minimum of Class 8 pass plus 18 years of age, while others require Class 10 pass with an ITI qualification, so requirements should be checked for the specific course you're interested in.
  • School dropouts and unemployed youth are a specific focus group for the scheme, alongside currently enrolled students in relevant linked categories (such as Kanyashree Class 11/12 students).

Documents Required

Prepare the following documents when registering for a PBSSD skill development course:

  • Aadhaar card.
  • Proof of West Bengal residence (Aadhaar, Voter ID, or Ration Card).
  • Educational qualification certificates/mark sheets relevant to the chosen course (Class 8/10/12 pass proof).
  • Passport-size photograph of the candidate.
  • Bank account details (for stipend-linked courses, where applicable).
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC), if applying under a reserved category benefit for a specific course.

Exact document requirements can vary depending on the training centre and the specific course selected, so it's best to confirm the full list with your chosen PBSSD-empanelled training provider at the time of registration.

How to Apply

Follow these steps to register for Utkarsh Bangla vocational training:

  1. Visit the official PBSSD portal (pbssd.gov.in) or your district's Utkarsh Bangla registration page (each district publishes its own registration link and list of available courses through the district administration website).
  2. Browse the list of available short-term skill development courses in your district — the number and type of courses vary by district and by training cycle.
  3. Register online, providing your personal, educational, and contact details.
  4. Select your preferred course and training centre location.
  5. Once registered, you'll be allocated to a PBSSD-empanelled training provider, who will contact you regarding batch scheduling and induction.
  6. Attend the training, which is tracked through biometric attendance at the centre, and complete the assessment and certification process at the end of the course.
  7. On successful completion, candidates receive certification and become eligible for placement support and access to linked employment schemes.

Since online registration windows can open and close depending on the training cycle and district-level targets, check your district's Utkarsh Bangla page regularly if registration isn't open at the time you check.

Training Quality Control, NSQF Alignment and Biometric Audits

To guarantee high instructional standards across all 23 districts, the Paschim Banga Society for Skill Development (PBSSD) implements strict quality management controls:

  • NSQF & Sector Skill Council Accreditation: All short-term courses are aligned with National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF) levels (Levels 3 to 6), ensuring standardized practical curricula across ITIs and private training hubs.
  • Time-Stamped Biometric Monitoring: Daily attendance of both trainers and students is captured via Aadhaar-enabled biometric attendance systems (AEBAS) to eliminate proxy attendance and verify course completion hours.
  • Third-Party Skill Assessment: Final practical assessments and theory examinations are conducted by independent Sector Skill Council (SSC) assessors before digital certificates are generated on the PBSSD portal.

Corporate Placement Melas, Apprenticeships and Entrepreneurship Loans

Utkarsh Bangla integrates skill certification with active employment facilitation infrastructure across West Bengal:

  • District Level Placement Fairs (Utkarsh Melas): The Department of Technical Education organizes quarterly job fairs in Kolkata, Siliguri, Asansol, and Kharagpur, bringing together top employers like Samsung, Tata Motors, and Berger Paints for direct campus hiring.
  • National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS): Certified candidates are onboarded onto the NAPS and West Bengal Apprenticeship portals, securing paid industry apprenticeships with monthly stipends.
  • Bhavishyat Credit Card & Self-Employment Capital: Trainees seeking self-employment in trades like tailoring, electrical repair, or beauty services are linked with the Bhavishyat Credit Card scheme for low-interest business loans up to ₹5 lakh.
