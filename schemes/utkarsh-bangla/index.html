Utkarsh Bangla — Free Skill Development & Job Placement Scheme

Utkarsh Bangla is a flagship skill development scheme launched by the Government of West Bengal on 16th February 2016, run under the Paschim Banga Society for Skill Development (PBSSD), Department of Technical Education, Training & Skill Development. It provides free, placement-linked short-term vocational training to youth and unemployed individuals across the state — helping them gain practical skills for employment or self-employment.

What is Utkarsh Bangla Scheme?

Utkarsh Bangla offers free-of-cost training courses ranging from 12 to 1200 hours, depending on the trade. Courses are delivered through PBSSD-empaneled Training Providers at registered Training Centres across all districts of West Bengal. On successful completion, candidates receive a certificate, and the scheme facilitates campus placement opportunities with partner companies.

Utkarsh Bangla Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be a resident of West Bengal
  • Open to youth and unemployed individuals, including school dropouts
  • Age criteria vary by specific course/trade — check individual course details before registering
  • Valid identity proof required (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License)
  • No prior technical qualification required for most entry-level trades

Courses & Training Offered

  • Driving
  • Tailoring
  • Electronics repair (TV and other appliances)
  • Beautician courses
  • Polytechnic and ITI-linked training
  • Many other trade-specific short-term courses, varying by district and training centre

Utkarsh Bangla Scheme Benefits

  • 100% free vocational training — no course fees
  • Training duration ranges from 12 hours (short orientation courses) up to 1200 hours (in-depth trade courses)
  • Certificate issued on successful course completion
  • Campus placement support — partner companies have included names like Samsung, Raymond, Berger Paints, Maruti Suzuki, and others across sectors
  • Around 6 lakh students trained annually through Polytechnic and ITI-linked programmes under the scheme

Official PBSSD / Utkarsh Bangla Portal Link

Official Portal: pbssd.gov.in

How to Register (Summary)

  1. Visit the official Utkarsh Bangla / PBSSD registration portal pbssd.gov.in
  2. Register with your basic personal details and mobile number
  3. Browse available short-term skill development courses in your district
  4. Select a course and your preferred Training Centre
  5. Complete registration and attend the training at the scheduled Training Venue
  6. Receive your certificate upon successful completion
