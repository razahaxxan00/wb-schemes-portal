West Bengal Government Housing Schemes

West Bengal runs housing assistance programs to help economically weaker families build permanent homes. Here's the full comparison of available schemes.

Schemes in This Category

Banglar Bari Prakalpa

₹1.20 lakh rural housing construction grant for EWS families living in kutcha houses.

Geetanjali Housing Scheme

Financial assistance (₹1.23L - ₹3.00L) to build or acquire a house for EWS/BPL families.
