Senior Citizen Welfare Schemes in West Bengal (2026)

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Complete guide to West Bengal government programmes for senior citizens — covering WCD Old Age Pension (Bardhakya Bhata), the Senior Citizen Card, state old age homes, healthcare coverage, and travel concessions.

Overview: What's Available to West Bengal's Senior Citizens

West Bengal offers a combination of direct financial support, identification-based concessions, healthcare access, and residential care options for its senior citizens (generally defined as residents aged 60 and above). Most direct financial support runs through the Jai Bangla pension umbrella, while a separate Senior Citizen Card unlocks a range of everyday concessions — travel, banking, and priority health access — once issued.

This page brings together what's currently available; each has its own eligibility rules and application process, summarised below. See also all Pension Schemes operating across West Bengal.

WCD Old Age Pension (Bardhakya Bhata) — the Core Benefit

The WCD Old Age Pension Scheme, commonly known in Bengali as Bardhakya Bhata, is West Bengal's primary direct pension for senior citizens, originally launched in 2010 and now operating under the state's broader Jai Bangla pension umbrella (unified since April 2020).

  • Eligibility: Permanent resident of West Bengal, aged 60 or above (55 for persons with physical or mental disabilities), with no other government pension being received, and generally required to have resided in the state for at least 10 years.
  • Monthly Amount: Historically Rs. 1,000/month, but multiple sources report a revision to Rs. 1,500/month effective 1 August 2026 under the 2026-27 state budget.
  • Payment Mode: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) via WBIFMS directly to the beneficiary's bank account.
  • Where to Apply: Collect and submit Form P at your local BDO office, Municipality office, or a Duare Sarkar camp.

→ Read the Jai Bangla Pension Scheme — full guide for the complete application process, documents required, and how to check your payment status.

The West Bengal Senior Citizen Card

Separate from the pension itself, residents aged 60+ can apply for a Senior Citizen Card, which serves as official recognition of senior status and unlocks access to a range of concessions and simplified enrolment in other welfare schemes:

  • Issued By: The District Social Welfare Officer, typically applied for through the WBRTPS (West Bengal Right to Public Services) portal or your local Social Welfare office.
  • Processing Time: Under the Right to Public Services Act, the card is required to be issued within 15 working days of a complete application.
  • Documents Needed: Age proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, or passport) showing you are 60 or above, proof of West Bengal residence, and passport-size photographs.
  • What It Unlocks: Discounted/priority travel on state transport, higher interest rates on certain bank savings and fixed deposit products, priority in health services, and easier enrolment into other senior-focused welfare programmes.

Old Age Homes

For seniors needing residential care, West Bengal supports old age homes through two channels:

  • Apanjan Home: A fully state government-funded and -run old age home located in South Garia, South 24 Parganas district, with capacity for 36 female and 32 male residents, providing food, clothing, toiletries, medicines, and recreational facilities.
  • IPSrC Homes Under AVYAY: Beyond Apanjan Home, West Bengal has around 30 additional old age homes operating under the Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens (IPSrC), part of the central Atal Vayo Abhyudaya Yojana (AVYAY) scheme. These are run by NGOs with central government funding, based on state government recommendation.

Health Coverage for Seniors

Health coverage for West Bengal's senior citizens currently runs through the state's broader health insurance framework, which has itself changed significantly in 2026 — West Bengal adopted the central Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) scheme from 1 July 2026, alongside a new state top-up, the Mukhyamantri Swasthya Bima Yojana (MMSBY), replacing the older Swasthya Sathi scheme. Senior citizens aged 70+ also qualify for an additional Rs. 5 lakh cover under Ayushman Vay Vandana Yojana.

→ Read the Swasthya Sathi / Ayushman Bharat transition guide for what this means for senior citizens specifically, and how to check your current coverage status.

Travel & Financial Concessions

Holding a valid Senior Citizen Card generally supports access to key concessions:

  • Bus travel concessions on West Bengal Transport Corporation (WBTC) services.
  • Railway ticket concessions, in line with national senior citizen travel guidelines.
  • Higher interest rates on savings accounts and fixed deposits offered by commercial and co-operative banks to senior citizen account holders.

How These Benefits Connect

Recommended action plan for senior citizens in West Bengal:

  1. Get your Senior Citizen Card first — it's the fastest route to simplified access to most of the concessions above, and the RTS Act's 15-working-day guarantee means it shouldn't take long.
  2. Apply for the Old Age Pension (Bardhakya Bhata) separately through your BDO/Municipality/Duare Sarkar camp — this is a distinct application from the Senior Citizen Card.
  3. Confirm your health coverage status under Ayushman Bharat/MMSBY, since this has recently changed from the older Swasthya Sathi system.
  4. If residential care is needed, contact your District Social Welfare Office to ask about availability at Apanjan Home or a nearby IPSrC-funded old age home.

Maintenance Tribunals under Maintenance of Parents & Senior Citizens Act

To protect vulnerable elderly residents from financial neglect, eviction, or property coercion by children or legal heirs:

  • Sub-Divisional Maintenance Tribunals: Senior citizens can file summary claims before the Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) Maintenance Tribunal for monthly maintenance allowances (up to ₹10,000 per month) from children or legal heirs.
  • Revocation of Property Transfers: Property conditional transfers executed by senior citizens can be declared void by the Tribunal if children fail to provide basic physical amenities and care.
  • Protection Against Forcible Eviction: Local police stations and District Magistrates are legally empowered to intervene and reinstate senior citizens facing illegal home eviction.

Samabyathi Prakalpa — Emergency Bereavement Financial Aid

To ensure dignified funeral rites for deceased senior citizens from impoverished families across West Bengal:

  • ₹2,000 One-Time Ex-Gratia Grant: Immediate cash assistance of ₹2,000 is disbursed to bereaved low-income family members or caretakers to perform cremation or burial rites.
  • On-the-Spot Disbursement: Processed through local Gram Panchayat offices, Municipalities, or cremation ground administrative counters upon presenting a death declaration.

Geriatric Care Wards and Tele-Medicine Helplines for Seniors

Specialized medical facilities are provided for elderly patients across medical colleges and district hospitals:

  • Dedicated Geriatric OPD Counters: Senior citizens receive priority registration, non-queue pharmacy counters, and dedicated OPD consultation desks in all state medical colleges.
  • Elder Line Helpline (14567): National toll-free helpline providing tele-counseling, legal advice, emergency rescue, and information on old age homes for senior citizens across West Bengal.

Comprehensive senior citizen welfare policies ensure that elderly residents across West Bengal enjoy financial security, quality medical care, legal protection, and social respect in their golden years.
