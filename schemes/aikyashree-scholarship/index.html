Aikyashree Scholarship — West Bengal Minority Students' Scholarship

Aikyashree is a West Bengal Government scholarship scheme run by the West Bengal Minorities' Development & Finance Corporation (WBMDFC). It provides financial assistance to students from the state's minority communities — Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, and Parsi — so that family income doesn't stand in the way of education. Aikyashree Scholarship covers students from the pre-matric school level all the way up to postgraduate and doctoral studies, through three categories: Pre-Matric, Post-Matric, and Merit-cum-Means (MCM).

What is Aikyashree Scholarship?

Aikyashree Scholarship is not a single fixed award — it's an umbrella scheme with three categories depending on the student's class or course:

  • Pre-Matric — for school students before Class 10
  • Post-Matric — for students from Class 11 up to postgraduate, diploma, ITI, and PhD level
  • Merit-cum-Means (MCM) — for students in professional and technical courses such as engineering, medicine, management, and law

All three categories are applied for through the same WBMDFC portal.

Aikyashree Scholarship Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be a permanent resident of West Bengal
  • Must belong to a notified minority community — Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, or Parsi
  • Must be currently enrolled in a recognised school, college, university, ITI, polytechnic, or madrasah in West Bengal
  • Family income must be within the ceiling set for the category (around ₹2 lakh/year for Post-Matric)
  • Minimum marks in the last qualifying exam — typically 50% for Post-Matric, around 75% for Merit-cum-Means
  • Students already receiving another government scholarship are usually not eligible for Aikyashree at the same time
  • Renewal each year requires maintaining the minimum marks/attendance required

Aikyashree Scholarship Amount Details

  • Pre-Matric: covers tuition-linked fees plus a maintenance allowance, amount depends on class
  • Post-Matric: around ₹5,500 – ₹16,500 per year, depending on course and whether the student is a day scholar or hosteller
  • Merit-cum-Means (MCM): up to ₹33,000 per year, covering tuition fees plus maintenance allowance

The scholarship amount is paid directly into the student's Aadhaar-linked bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

How to Apply for Aikyashree Scholarship

  1. Visit the official WBMDFC / Aikyashree scholarship portal wbmdfcscholarship.in
  2. Choose Fresh Registration (new applicant) or Renewal (returning scholar), or log in if already registered
  3. Select your district, then fill in personal, academic, and bank account details
  4. Upload the required documents
  5. Submit the form and note your Application ID for tracking later

Documents required: minority/community certificate, income certificate, previous mark sheet, Aadhaar card, bank passbook, passport-size photo, and institution/bonafide certificate.

Official Portal & Helpline

Official WBMDFC Portal: wbmdfcscholarship.in

Toll-Free Helpline: 1800-120-2130

Aikyashree Scholarship Last Date

Aikyashree does not have one single deadline for the whole scheme. Fresh applications, renewals, Post-Matric, MCM, and TSP each get their own last date, announced by WBMDFC at the start of each academic year. Application cycles have typically opened between January and March. Check the official WBMDFC notice for the current year's exact last date, as it changes every cycle.
