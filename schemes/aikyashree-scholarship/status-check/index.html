How to Check Your Aikyashree Scholarship Status

You can check your Aikyashree Scholarship application status online in a few minutes using your district and Application ID. This guide covers checking application status, status by Application ID, and payment status.

How to Check Aikyashree Scholarship Status Online

  1. Go to the official Aikyashree / WBMDFC scholarship portal wbmdfcscholarship.in
  2. Find "Track Your Application" on the portal homepage or student panel
  3. Select your District — the one where your school/college/institution is located
  4. Enter your Application ID / Reference Number, generated when you first submitted the application
  5. Submit to view your current status: under verification, approved, sent for disbursement, or paid

Official Application Tracking Portal

Tracking Link: wbmdfcscholarship.in

Checking Status by Application ID

Your Application ID is the reference number issued when your registration was submitted, and is normally shown on-screen and sent to your registered mobile number/email. If you've lost it, log back into the student panel using your registered mobile number to retrieve it.

Checking Payment Status

Scholarship money is paid by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly to the student's Aadhaar-linked bank account. Application status and payment status are tracked through the same portal login. If your status shows "approved" but the amount hasn't arrived after a reasonable processing time, check your bank account's Aadhaar linkage before assuming it's just delayed.

Troubleshooting FAQs

Double-check the district selected matches your institution's location — this is the most common reason a valid ID shows no result. If it still doesn't work, contact the WBMDFC helpline.

Confirm your bank account is Aadhaar-linked and the account details in your application are correct.

Log in to the student panel with your registered mobile number to retrieve your application details.

WBMDFC helpline — toll-free 1800-120-2130.
