Lakshmir Bhandar Scheme — West Bengal Women's Monthly Assistance

Lakshmir Bhandar was a monthly cash transfer scheme for women from West Bengal households, launched in 2021. As of June 2026, the scheme has been restructured and renamed to Annapurna Bhandar by the state government, with a higher monthly amount. Existing Lakshmir Bhandar beneficiaries do not need to reapply — they are being migrated automatically.

📢 2026 Update: Transition to Annapurna Bhandar

Following the 2026 West Bengal Assembly election, the newly formed state government introduced Annapurna Bhandar as the successor to Lakshmir Bhandar, with rollout from June 2026:

  • Monthly amount increased to ₹3,000 for all eligible women, removing the earlier General/SC-ST split
  • Existing Lakshmir Bhandar beneficiaries are being auto-migrated after a re-verification exercise, without needing a fresh application in most cases
  • A window was opened for women not previously enrolled to apply fresh
  • Some previously enrolled women were removed after an eligibility audit (commonly for reasons like government employment, pension, or broken Aadhaar-bank linkage)

If your Lakshmir Bhandar payment has stopped, the most likely reasons are a failed re-verification, an Aadhaar-bank linking issue, or a change in employment/pension status — check your status using the guide below before assuming the scheme has ended.

What is Lakshmir Bhandar Scheme?

Lakshmir Bhandar (লক্ষ্মীর ভান্ডার) provided monthly direct benefit transfer (DBT) payments to eligible women from economically weaker households in West Bengal, aimed at women aged 25 to 60 who were not government employees, pensioners, or income-tax payers. At its peak, around 2.2 crore women were enrolled.

Lakshmir Bhandar Eligibility Criteria (as it stood under the scheme)

  • Woman aged 25 to 60 years
  • Permanent resident of West Bengal
  • Not a government employee, pensioner, or income-tax payer
  • General category: household should not own more than 2 hectares of agricultural land
  • Household enrolled in Swasthya Sathi

Lakshmir Bhandar Monthly Amount Details

Under Lakshmir Bhandar, General category women received ₹1,500 per month and SC/ST category women received ₹1,700 per month, paid via DBT into an Aadhaar-linked bank account.
