Status & Payment Tracking

How to Check Your Lakshmir Bhandar / Annapurna Bhandar Status

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

You can track your Lakshmir Bhandar (now Annapurna Bhandar) application or monthly payment status online. This guide covers checking status online and what to do if your payment hasn't arrived.

How to Check Lakshmir Bhandar Status Online

  1. Visit the official West Bengal Social Security / Annapurna Bhandar portal socialsecurity.wb.gov.in
  2. Look for "Track Applicant & View Payment Status" on the homepage
  3. Enter your Application ID, Swasthya Sathi card number, or registered mobile number when prompted
  4. Submit to view your current status — under verification, approved, or paid

Official Status Tracking Portal

Tracking Link: socialsecurity.wb.gov.in

Checking Status by Application Number

Your application number was issued when you first registered for Lakshmir Bhandar and is required for tracking on the portal. If you don't have it, your Swasthya Sathi card number or registered mobile number can usually be used instead to look up your record.

Lakshmir Bhandar Payment Not Received — What to Check

If your status shows approved but payment hasn't arrived, check these common causes before contacting your local office:

  • Aadhaar not properly linked/seeded with your bank account
  • Name mismatch between your bank account and application (even a single character can cause a failed transfer)
  • Swasthya Sathi enrolment lapsed or expired
  • Household was flagged during the 2026 re-verification exercise for the Annapurna Bhandar transition

Troubleshooting FAQs

Confirm your Aadhaar-bank linkage is active and your name matches exactly across Aadhaar, bank account, and application. If both are correct, the delay is usually cleared in the next payment cycle.

Use your registered mobile number or Swasthya Sathi card number on the portal instead.

Most commonly this is due to the 2026 re-verification exercise — visit your local BDO/Municipality office or gram panchayat with your documents to raise a correction if you believe it's an error.

Visit your nearest Duare Sarkar camp, BDO office, or the helpline number listed on the official portal.
