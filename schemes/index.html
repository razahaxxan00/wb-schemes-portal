Krishak Bandhu Scheme

Annual financial assistance & death benefit insurance for farmers.

Swasthya Sathi Scheme

Cashless health insurance cover up to ₹5 lakh per family per year.

Aikyashree Scholarship

Scholarship program for minority community students (Pre, Post, MCM).

Khadya Sathi Scheme

Subsidized rice & wheat at ₹2/kg for eligible ration cardholders.

Lakshmir Bhandar

Monthly cash assistance for women (transitioning to Annapurna Bhandar).

Bangla Shasya Bima

100% free crop insurance scheme protecting farmers against crop loss.

Rupashree Prakalpa

One-time grant of ₹25,000 for adult daughters' marriage.

Jai Bangla Pension Scheme

Unified monthly pension scheme (₹1,000/month) for elderly, widows & workers.

Sabooj Sathi Scheme

Free bicycles for Class 9 to 12 students in government & aided schools.

Kanyashree Prakalpa

Scholarships (K1, K2, K3) for girl students from Class VIII through postgraduate.

Manabik Pension Scheme

Monthly pension for persons with disabilities.

Samabyathi Prakalpa

One-time ₹2,000 cash funeral assistance for poor families.

Shikshashree Scheme

Pre-matric scholarship for SC/ST students in Class V to VIII.

Geetanjali Housing Scheme

Housing construction assistance for EWS/BPL families.
