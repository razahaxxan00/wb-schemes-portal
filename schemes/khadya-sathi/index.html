Khadya Sathi Scheme — Subsidised Rice & Wheat for West Bengal Families

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wb.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

Khadya Sathi (খাদ্য সাথী, "Food Companion") is a West Bengal Government food security scheme run by the Department of Food & Supplies. Launched on 27 January 2016, it provides rice and wheat at ₹2 per kg to eligible ration cardholders, covering an estimated 90% of the state's population.

What is Khadya Sathi Scheme?

Khadya Sathi ensures every ration cardholder in West Bengal has access to subsidised food grains through a uniform public distribution system. The scheme covers Below Poverty Line (BPL) and Economically Weaker Section (EWS) families, Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) households, SC/ST communities, tea garden workers, and residents of cyclone-affected and Jangalmahal areas. Grains are collected from ration shops using a digital/e-ration card linked to Aadhaar.

Khadya Sathi Scheme Benefits

  • Rice and wheat (or fortified atta) at ₹2 per kg for most ration card categories
  • Monthly entitlement varies by card type:
    • AAY cardholders: around 21 kg rice + 13.3 kg atta (or 14 kg wheat) per family per month
    • PHH/SPHH (Priority/State Priority) cardholders: around 3 kg rice + 1.9 kg atta (or 2 kg wheat) per head per month
    • RKSY-I cardholders: around 2 kg rice + 3 kg wheat per head per month
  • Coverage across ordinary households, tea garden families, homeless persons, and cyclone/disaster-affected residents
  • Digital e-ration card usable at any ration shop in the state, reducing paperwork and leakage

Khadya Sathi Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be a permanent resident of West Bengal
  • Must hold a valid ration card (digital/e-ration card) issued by the WB Food & Supplies Department
  • Priority given to BPL, EWS, AAY, SC/ST families, tea garden workers, and residents of Jangalmahal, Purulia, and cyclone-affected regions
  • Government employees are generally excluded from certain subsidised categories
  • Applicant and family members must be Indian citizens

How to Apply

  1. Visit the West Bengal Food & Supplies Department portal wbpds.wb.gov.in or the Egiye Bangla portal
  2. Select the Khadya Sathi / ration card beneficiary section and choose "New Applicant"
  3. Verify your mobile number with OTP
  4. Fill in family details and upload documents — Aadhaar card, Voter ID, income certificate, and passport-size photo
  5. Submit online, or apply offline at your nearest ration shop or Duare Sarkar camp

Once approved, your digital ration card (Khadya Sathi card) is issued and linked to your Aadhaar for biometric verification at the ration shop.
