How to Download Your Khadya Sathi Ration Card

Your Khadya Sathi e-ration card can be downloaded online from the West Bengal Food & Supplies Department portal. This guide covers downloading your card and getting a duplicate copy if it's lost.

How to Download Khadya Sathi Ration Card Online

  1. Visit the official Food & Supplies Department portal wbpds.wb.gov.in
  2. Go to the Khadya Sathi / e-Ration Card section
  3. Log in or verify using your ration card number, Aadhaar number, or registered mobile number with OTP
  4. Select "Download Card" or "Print e-Ration Card"
  5. Save or print the PDF — this digital copy is valid for use at ration shops

Official e-Ration Card Download Portal

Download Portal: wbpds.wb.gov.in

Getting a Khadya Sathi Duplicate Card

If your original ration card is lost or damaged, you can generate a duplicate through the same portal by verifying your identity with your ration card number or Aadhaar-linked mobile number. There's no need to visit an office in person for a straightforward duplicate download — an e-ration card printout is accepted at ration shops just like the original.
