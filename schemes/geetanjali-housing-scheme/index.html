Geetanjali Housing Scheme — Housing Assistance for the Homeless (2026)

Fact-Checked 2026 Fact-checked against official state notifications (wbhousing.gov.in) • Editorial Review: August 2026

The Geetanjali Housing Scheme (also called Geetanjali Awas Yojana) is West Bengal's state-funded housing initiative dedicated to providing permanent shelter and construction grants for homeless, landless, and Economically Weaker Section (EWS) households.

What Is the Geetanjali Housing Scheme

The Geetanjali Housing Scheme (also written Gitanjali, and sometimes called Geetanjali Awas Yojana) is a housing assistance programme run by the Government of West Bengal for homeless and economically weaker families who don't own a pucca (permanent, brick-and-mortar) house. The scheme's stated motto is "Sabar Mathar Upare Chhad" — a roof over everyone's head.

On 1 April 2014, the West Bengal government merged an earlier, similar scheme called "Amar Thikana" into Geetanjali, so that housing assistance for the state's poorest households would run under a single, unified scheme implemented through the District Magistrates of each district, under the West Bengal Housing Department. The scheme places special focus on the Sundarban and Paschimanchal (western region) areas, as well as families belonging to the Minority Community and fishermen communities, alongside the general EWS/BPL population.

Objective of the Scheme

The primary objectives of the Geetanjali Housing Scheme include:

  • Provide Permanent Shelter: Provide permanent shelter to Economically Weaker Section (EWS) and Below Poverty Line (BPL) families who don't have a pucca house registered in the name of any family member.
  • State-Wide Rural & Urban Coverage: Cover both rural and urban areas of West Bengal, since the scheme applies statewide rather than being restricted to villages or cities alone.
  • Disaster Rehabilitation: Rehabilitate families displaced by natural calamities, such as floods or erosion, or by government rehabilitation projects.
  • Urban Group Housing for Landless: Support urban homeless individuals who don't own land, by constructing group housing on land provided by the district administration, municipality, or development authority where individual land isn't available.
  • Local Employment Generation: Generate local employment for construction workers as part of the housing construction process.

Financial Assistance Amount

Reported figures for Geetanjali vary depending on the source and the period being referenced, so treat the numbers below as indicative rather than fixed, and confirm current rates with your District Magistrate's office or the West Bengal Housing Department before applying:

  • IAY-Pattern Baseline Rates: Under the scheme's IAY-pattern (Indira Awaas Yojana-aligned) construction assistance, historical rates have been around Rs. 70,000 per unit in plain rural and urban areas, and Rs. 75,000 per unit in hilly, difficult, or Sundarban areas.
  • Revised Unit Range: Some more recent listings describe a wider assistance range, from roughly Rs. 1,23,000 to Rs. 3,00,000, depending on the beneficiary's category and location — this likely reflects revisions and inflation adjustments made to the scheme over time.
  • Installment-Based Release: Assistance is typically released in installments — commonly a first instalment of around 70% of the sanctioned amount, followed by the remaining balance after a utilisation certificate and physical progress report are submitted.
  • Free Urban Group Housing: In some fully urban cases (for example, under Kolkata Municipal Corporation area rollouts), completed housing units have reportedly been provided at zero cost to the most severely deprived beneficiaries, such as footpath dwellers, rather than as a cash construction grant.

Because assistance amounts and the exact urban/rural mechanism can differ by district and have been revised over the years, always verify the current sanctioned amount for your specific category with the local housing office before budgeting around it.

Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for housing support under Geetanjali, applicants must meet the following eligibility conditions:

  • The applicant must be a permanent resident of West Bengal.
  • The applicant must belong to an Economically Weaker Section (EWS) or Below Poverty Line (BPL) household — some listings cite a monthly household income ceiling of roughly Rs. 6,000 or less, though this should be confirmed locally.
  • The applicant must not currently own a pucca house in the name of any family member.
  • For rural construction, the beneficiary generally needs to have hassle-free land of their own (or patta land) on which to build, free of encumbrances.
  • For urban areas where the applicant has no land, the scheme covers group housing built on land provided by the district administration, municipality, or development authority.
  • Victims of natural calamities (floods, erosion) or families displaced by government rehabilitation projects are also eligible, even if they don't otherwise meet the standard income criteria.

Documents Required

Prepare the following documents when submitting your Geetanjali housing application:

  • Aadhaar card.
  • Voter ID card (EPIC).
  • Income certificate (issued with Gram Panchayat or competent government authority approval).
  • Proof of current residence in West Bengal.
  • Ration card.
  • Ethnic/community certificate, if applicable (for BPL, SC, ST, or minority category applicants).
  • Two passport-size photographs.
  • Land ownership document or patta document (for rural applicants building on their own land).

How to Apply

Applications can be processed either offline or online:

Offline Method:

  1. Visit the office of your Gram Panchayat, Block Development Officer (BDO), Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO), District Magistrate, or Municipal Corporation, depending on your area.
  2. Collect the Geetanjali Housing Scheme application form.
  3. Fill in your personal, income, and land/housing details, and attach the required documents.
  4. Submit the completed form to the designated office and collect an acknowledgment receipt.

Online Method:

  1. Visit the official West Bengal Housing Department portal: wbhousing.gov.in.
  2. Register and select the Geetanjali Housing Scheme option under beneficiary services.
  3. Complete the application form and upload scanned copies of the required documents.
  4. Submit the form online and retain your application reference number for tracking.

Multi-Departmental Rural Implementation Channels

A unique feature of the Geetanjali Housing Scheme is its decentralized implementation strategy across specialized state departments:

  • Sundarban & Paschimanchal Affairs: Targeted housing construction grants tailored for vulnerable coastal and drought-prone western districts.
  • Fisheries & Forest Departments: Dedicated housing allocations for recognized traditional fishermen and forest-dwelling tribal communities.
  • Minority Affairs & Backward Classes Welfare: Direct unit quotas for EWS households from minority, SC, and ST demographic groups managed via District Welfare Officers.

Technical Inspection, Utilisation Certificates & Geo-Tagging

To ensure quality construction and prevent fund misuse, project disbursements are linked to strict physical milestones:

  • Lintel & Plinth Level Verification: Block Assistant Engineers inspect house construction at plinth and lintel levels before authorizing second installment releases.
  • Geo-Tagged Photographic Proof: Beneficiaries and block technical officers upload geo-tagged photos of the under-construction dwelling to the Housing Department portal.
  • Utilisation Certificate (UC) Submission: Final payment releases require a formal UC signed by the applicant and certified by the Gram Panchayat Executive Assistant or Municipal Inspector.
