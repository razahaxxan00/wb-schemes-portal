Geetanjali Housing Scheme — Housing Assistance for the Economically Weaker Sections

Geetanjali Housing Scheme (also called Geetanjali Awas Yojana) is a West Bengal Government housing scheme, run by the Department of Housing, that provides financial assistance to build or acquire a home for economically weaker section (EWS) and Below Poverty Line (BPL) families who don't already own a pucca house. It replaced the earlier "Amar Thikana" scheme from 1 April 2014.

What is Geetanjali Housing Scheme?

The scheme covers both rural and urban West Bengal. In rural areas, it's implemented through six departments depending on the beneficiary's category — Minority Affairs & Madrasah Education, Backward Classes Welfare, Fisheries, Forest, Sundarban Affairs, and Paschimanchal Unnayan Affairs. In urban areas, constructed housing units are provided to homeless families who don't own land. The scheme also covers families who lost their homes to natural calamities (like floods) or government rehabilitation projects.

Geetanjali Housing Scheme Eligibility

  • Must be a permanent resident of West Bengal
  • Must belong to an Economically Weaker Section (EWS) or Below Poverty Line (BPL) family
  • Should not already own a pucca house in the name of any family member
  • For rural applicants building on their own land: must have valid land ownership documents
  • For urban applicants without land: eligible for a fully constructed housing unit instead
  • Priority given to families affected by natural disasters (floods, erosion) or displaced by government projects

Geetanjali Housing Scheme Assistance Amount

Financial assistance to construct a house ranges from approximately ₹1,23,000 to ₹3,00,000, depending on the beneficiary's category and location (plains vs. hills/difficult/coastal areas attract different unit costs). The amount is typically released in two instalments — the first half upfront, with the remainder released after a utilisation certificate and physical progress report are submitted.

Official Housing Department Link

Official Portal: wbhousing.gov.in

How to Apply

  1. Visit your local Block Development Office (BDO) for rural areas, or Municipality/KMC office for urban areas
  2. Collect and fill the Geetanjali Housing Scheme application form
  3. Attach required documents — Aadhaar card, voter ID, income certificate, land ownership proof (for rural construction), and passport-size photographs
  4. Submit at the designated office and keep the acknowledgement receipt
  5. Applications can also be submitted online through the West Bengal Housing Department portal (wbhousing.gov.in)
